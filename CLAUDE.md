## Standing rule: do not be Eurocentric
Indigenous knowledge and the old civilizations of Africa, India, China, the Americas, and elsewhere carry just as much weight as European sources. When a resource credits a saying or idea to a non-European tradition, look for that source and keep it alongside any European source; never drop or "correct" it to a European origin for lack of a quick citation. (Coral, November 11, 2026, after E924 and E943.)
