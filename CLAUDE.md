## Standing rule: do not be Eurocentric
Indigenous knowledge and the old civilizations of Africa, India, China, the Americas, and elsewhere carry just as much weight as European sources. When a resource credits a saying or idea to a non-European tradition, look for that source and keep it alongside any European source; never drop or "correct" it to a European origin for lack of a quick citation. (Coral, November 11, 2026, after E924 and E943.)

## Standing rule: source every general summary
Any resource that reads like a general summary (often Coral's own search summaries) gets a real source found and proposed, so the wording can be checked against it and corrected if needed. Do not leave such notes unmarked or unsourced. (Coral, November 11, 2026.)

## Standing rule: trim generic interpretive passages
The book already runs about 1,400 pages. In each batch, propose trims (E items with Current / Proposed / Why) for long resource passages: keep facts, sources, quotations, and observations tied to a line in Young's text; cut sentences that only restate the novel's big themes in general terms ("reflects the novel's themes of illusion and reality," "the boundary between life and death," "the interplay of light and shadow"). Never trim Coral's own arguments or views (for example on racism, slavery, the Magical Negro trope, the white suffrage movement). Nothing is cut without her OK. (Coral, November 11, 2026.)

## Standing rules for A Guide to Reading Miss MacIntosh, My Darling
(Coral, November 12, 2026, E1490 to E1497.)
- Every quotation from the novel is exact, word for word. A paraphrase never goes in quotation marks.
- No dashes in Coral's own writing. Dashes inside quotations from the novel or any other source stay.
- Everything the Guide says happens must match what happens in the novel. In each chapter pass, check every plot statement (who is where, who says what, what happens) against the novel text and propose corrections (Coral, November 12, 2026, after E1523 and E1524: old Doc is not on the bus).
- For the Curious is set for the Read-Along 2028; do not add, remove, or rename entries. Keep additions few; the research pages hold the rest.
- Guide change items are numbered G1, G2, G3, ... (Coral, November 12, 2026). E numbers stay for To All My Darlings and the rest of the site. The next G number is in TODO.md.
- No volume divisions. Miss MacIntosh, My Darling was published as one complete book; the two-volume editions were a later publishing decision, so the Guide does not say where a "Volume 1" or "Volume 2" begins or ends.
