Chapter Fourteen: Here Is Also Then

← Chapter Thirteen: The Witness Across Time · Radical Immanence Collection · Epilogue: The Wheel Holds →

Return to the field in central Indiana.

The soil is still the color of oxidized glacial till. The glaciers are still twelve thousand years gone and still present in the compression of the layers. The people who stood here before you are still in the sediment, still in the water table, still in the language you are using to think about them. The people who will stand here after you are still not yet born, still not yet formed, still being shaped by what is being deposited now, in this moment, by the decisions being made in this century about what to extract and what to leave and what to pass forward.

You are standing at the crossing point. You have always been standing at the crossing point. The crossing point has not moved.

What has changed, in the course of this book, is not your location. You were always here. What has changed is the legibility of here: the visibility of the structure you are inside, the palpability of the two axes and their intersection, the recognition of the transmission line running through you from the ancestors in the sediment to the not-yet-born in the not-yet-formed ground.

Here is not only here. Here is also the accumulated weight of everything that made here possible: the glacial till and the prairie grass and the people who stood here before you and the language they deposited in the sediment and the grief they carried and the buildings they built and the ground they formed and the weight of all of it pressing up through the soles of your feet.

Here is also the not-yet-existent weight of everything here will make possible: the ground being formed now by what is being deposited now, the sediment of the present becoming the substrate of the future, the alluvial material of this century becoming the till that the not-yet-born will stand on and read and be constituted by.

Here is enough. The tenth pillar of Radical Immanence establishes this: the sacred without the beyond, the ordinary world charged with a significance that points not outward to a transcendent ground but deeper into the immanent one. Here is enough because here is the whole of it, the plural self and the constructed real and the permanent grief and the witness and the recursive construction and the ethics of incompletion and the ontology of the marginal and the sedimentary time and the honest language and the immanent sacred.

Here is also enormous. The eleventh pillar adds this: here is not only the lateral spread of the present moment, the horizontal accumulation of the ten pillars. Here is also the vertical line of the transmission, the crossing point where the ancestors in the sediment meet the not-yet-born in the not-yet-formed ground. Here is the intersection of everything that was given and everything that will be required. Here is the crossing point.

The sentence hasn't ended. The ten pillars established this, and the eleventh pillar deepens it: the sentence hasn't ended because the transmission hasn't ended, because the wheel is still turning, because the crossing point is still holding the intersection of the horizontal and the vertical, because here is still also then, because the not-yet-born are still being formed by what is being deposited now, because the ancestors are still in the sediment, because the carrying continues.

The crossing point does not move. The wheel turns around it. The self at the crossing point is not going anywhere. It is turning, oriented now one way and now another, but always rotating around the same center, which is here, which is the intersection of everything it has received and everything it is passing forward, which is the most precisely located thing there is.

Here is enough. Here is also enormous. Here is the crossing point.

That is the eleventh pillar. That is what Young's sentence was always reaching toward. That is what the wheel was always turning around. That is what the cross was always marking.

You are here. Here is also then. The carrying continues.
