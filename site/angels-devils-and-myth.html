Definition from research

Chapter 5 · Four Passengers / Angels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John)

  • Suggest a spiritual or religious dimension to the bus-driver’s journey, hinting at judgment or salvation.

Chapter 19 · Chained Bible

  • Firm but restrained faith; tethering spirituality to reality.

Chapter 20 · Biblical and Mythological References

  • References to biblical figures (Saul, Solomon, Sennacherib) and ancient measurements, calendars, and places (Nisan, Jyar, Wady Feiran, Egypt to Canaan) ground the narrative in a spiritual and temporal history.
  • Quotes and allusions to Ovid and Greek perspectives on beauty and hair enhance the classical depth of the novel.

Chapter 21 · Valley of Dry Bones

  • Represents death, decay, and the desolation of existence, yet also possibly hints at resurrection or hope in a biblical sense.

Chapter 21 · The Devil as "God’s ape"

  • Illustrates the deceptive nature of evil and the difficulty in distinguishing it from good.

Chapter 21 · The Devil and Deception

  • This metaphor warns against being deceived by appearances, a central theme in the chapter.

Chapter 29 · Names and Mythology

  • Butterflies named after gods (Aphrodite, Apollo, Morpheus, Leto), mythological figures (Io, Atlas), and symbolic concepts.
  • These names embed butterflies in a rich symbolic and cultural context.

Chapter 29 · Butterfly Names and Mythological Associations

  • The rich tapestry of cultural and mythological references deepened the symbolism.

Chapter 37 · The Cross and Rose

  • Symbolize transformation and the intertwining of suffering and beauty, life and death.

Chapter 38 · Centaur & Stone Huntsmen

  • Mythical figures representing the intersection of past and present, reality and dream.

Chapter 39 · Dragons, Saints, and Mythical Creatures

  • Mythic battles represent internal struggles with despair, identity, and societal constraints.

Chapter 40 · Angelic and Mythical Figures (e.g., angels, unicorns, griffins)

  • Symbolize transcendence and the mingling of reality and fantasy.

Chapter 41 · Animals and Mythical Creatures

  • Elephants, rabbits in lace veils, falcons, and spectral figures populate Catherine’s dream world, symbolizing various aspects of memory, power, and mystery.

Chapter 65 · The Watch and Biblical Quotations

  • Mr. Bonebreaker’s watch symbolizes time running out, the urgency of salvation, and the inevitability of the end.
