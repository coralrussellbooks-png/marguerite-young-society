The old blind sheep dog on three legs (Ch 21)

From the reading guide, Chapter 21 · Research: Chapter 21

From the guide

The old blind sheep dog on three legs appears again at the chapter’s opening, running ahead through the surf, barking at sticks and shells and the carapace of dead animals, guarding a pasture that no longer exists against a thief who will not come. Young has placed this dog at several key moments in the sequence: barking at the waves at the end of Chapter 15, present again now at the return to ordinary life. A blind dog doing its job faithfully in the absence of any threat, in the presence of nothing to guard. It is one of Young’s most affectionate images of the faithful and the futile continuing side by side.
