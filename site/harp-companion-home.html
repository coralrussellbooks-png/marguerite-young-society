How to use this companion

This companion identifies the people, events, and terms in pages 1 to 581 of Marguerite Young's Harp Song for a Radical (Knopf, 1999), in twenty-one units that follow the book's page order. Page numbers are those of the Knopf first edition.

Each unit has three tiers:

  • Major entries give what the subject was, why it matters in American history, how Young uses it, where her account differs from the historical record, and one book for going further.

  • Shorter entries give two or three sentences on secondary figures and period details.

  • Allusions give one line on literary, biblical, and classical references.
