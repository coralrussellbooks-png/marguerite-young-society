On the pale narcissus.

Narcissus is the genus that includes daffodils: bulb flowers that push through frozen or rocky ground in early spring, often the first color after winter. The image Vera reaches for, a pale narcissus breaking through rock, is an image of insistence: not triumph, not glory, just the quiet, determined push of a living thing through resistance. This is what she sees in Miss MacIntosh. Not the wig or the umbrella or the practical lectures. The flower coming up through stone.
