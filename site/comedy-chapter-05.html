Definition from research

Moses is the chapter’s comedian, and his hair is his best material. He will never let it be cut, and he explains, with drunken logic, that he is hanging on to life by a single hair, and that if things get too bad he can always hang himself by it, which is at least something to be grateful for. He told the barber, who was going bald himself, that he would never touch it, “even as a joke.”

His politics run the same way. The Democrats are running the country into its grave, though he never has time to vote, and he disclaims all responsibility for the flooded roads and vanished signposts: he did not create the world or the distance between one place and another, so complaints should go to the company. The comedy is a man in charge of a bus who insists he is in charge of nothing, and it sits right beside the chapter’s darker question of whether he is driving at all.
