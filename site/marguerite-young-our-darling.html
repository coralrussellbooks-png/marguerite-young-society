Marguerite Young, Our Darling: Tributes and Essays

← Politics in Young's Works · The Marguerite Young Society · Young's Interviews Analyzed →

Published: Dalkey Archive Press, 1994, edited by Miriam Fuchs. 143 pages. Out of print; used copies can be found.

A collection gathered around Young and her work: about a dozen essays on her writing and influence, tributes from Amy Clampitt, Anne Tyler and Stanley Kunitz, and two interviews with Young herself.
