On Lucie Bell

Lucie Bell: the silent girl the stone deaf man loved, who loved a jukebox and a tired marathon skater more than him, who made too much noise and was not his love because she could not answer him: is the novel’s final figure of missed connection. She is gone before Vera arrives. The man calls her name into the silence he lives in. Vera hears his calling and falls in love with him instead. This is how the novel ends: not with the person who was sought but with the person who was called for by someone else, in a language that could not be heard by the one being addressed, and was heard anyway by someone passing through. Connection happens obliquely, or it happens in the gap between the person and their target, or it happens because silence carries things that sound cannot.
