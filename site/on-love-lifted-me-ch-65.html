On "Love Lifted Me."

The 1912 hymn Mr. Bonebreaker sings was written by James Rowe and draws on Matthew 14: the story of Peter walking on water, beginning to sink, and being caught by Jesus. The hymn is about being pulled from drowning by divine love. That Mr. Bonebreaker sings this to a woman who will eventually walk into the ocean is one of Young's characteristically quiet ironic placements. The hymn promises rescue. The novel does not guarantee it.
