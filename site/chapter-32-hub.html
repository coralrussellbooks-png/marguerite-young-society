Chapter 32: Weddings Are Funerals, and Funerals Are Weddings

← Chapter 31 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 33 →

Story arc: Arc 4 Cousin Hannah

Length: Medium, about 0.8% of the novel

Character hubs: Vera Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh · Catherine Cartwheel · Joachim Spitzer · Cousin Hannah · Madge Capehorn · The Black Coachman · Jackie White

Summary

Catherine remembers Hannah as a storm of a woman with a saber scar and burning eyes, who tried to break up Catherine's wedding by beating the bridegroom with her white umbrella and carrying the bride away. Catherine's marriage itself is spectral: a faceless bridegroom, a wedding to a sailboat, a porpoise, a star, a church bell. Hannah died old and alone and never married, though Catherine believes she still visits. Catherine always wanted to die while traveling, believing that death comes from partial knowledge.
