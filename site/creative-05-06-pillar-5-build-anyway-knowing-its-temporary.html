Pillar: Pillar 5 Build Anyway, Knowing It's Temporary · Across every book

What have you been calling natural that was actually built by someone?

The hunger is for the given order, the arrangement you did not build and need not question. Power loves the appeal to how things have always been. This pillar says it was all made, and what was made was made by hands like yours. That returns the dizziness of responsibility, but also the possibility of building it otherwise.

Notice

Notice one rule or arrangement in your life, at work, in your family, in your culture, that you have quietly treated as just how things are. Sit with the discomfort of asking who actually decided it.

Practice

Pick one thing you have called natural and trace its actual history. Who built it, when, and for what reason. You do not need a full research project, a rough sketch is enough to see that it was a decision, not a fact of nature.

Reflection

What would it take to build this arrangement differently?

Who benefits from this arrangement staying invisible, as though nobody made it?
