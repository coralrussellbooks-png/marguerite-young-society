Section 4. The Carriers: References Grouped by Character

Part of Intertextuality in Miss MacIntosh, My Darling, a study of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965).
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

Characters: Catherine Cartwheel · Cousin Hannah · Esther Longtree · Homer Capehorn · Joachim Spitzer · Madge Capehorn · Miss MacIntosh · Mr. Titus Bonebreaker · Mrs. Hogden · Vera Cartwheel

Allusions: Iowa, Indiana, and the Midwest

Definition from research

Every reference in the novel has a carrier, the character whose voice or mind produces it. Read one carrier at a time, the references stop looking like a scattered hoard and start looking like portraits. Each person in the book holds the Western inheritance in a way that belongs to that person alone. What they reach for, and how they hold it, tells as much about them as anything they do. This section takes the carriers one by one, beginning with the three who carry the most.
