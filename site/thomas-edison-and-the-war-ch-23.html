Thomas Edison and the war (Ch 23)

From the reading guide, Chapter 23 · Research: Chapter 23

From the guide

Thomas Edison and the war of the currents. Miss MacIntosh would like a word with Edison, and there was a genuine war over the lighting of the night. Through the late 1880s Edison's direct current system fought George Westinghouse's alternating current for the American market. Edison's side publicized fatal accidents from high-voltage AC lines, funded a campaign to prove AC was lethal, and arranged for the first electric chair to be powered by a Westinghouse generator. By 1892 Edison Electric had merged with its rival Thomson-Houston to form General Electric and the war was over. The last direct current utility in New York City was not shut down until 2007. The bright lights Miss MacIntosh objects to were installed over corpses, which is very nearly her point.
