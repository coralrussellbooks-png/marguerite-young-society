On deathbed conversion.

The possibility of a deathbed conversion, of turning to or returning to a faith at the last moment, hovers over Chapter 43 without quite landing. Cousin Hannah may have moved from Christianity to Islam to atheism and back through many positions across her long life and travels. What she calls out for in the delirium is not God exactly but love: the blackamoor, the guide, the lamp-lighter who might light her way. If this is a religious appeal, it is one stripped of all institutional content and reduced to its most fundamental form: the need for a companion at the crossing, for something that goes first, for a hand.
