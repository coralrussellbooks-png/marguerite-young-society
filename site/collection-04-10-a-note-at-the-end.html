A Note at the End

← Part Eight: The Crossing Point, Seven Days · Radical Immanence Collection

Here Is Enough said: the present moment is where your life is located. Come back to it. It is enough. You are enough, exactly as you are, right now.

The Crossing Point adds: the present moment is also where the transmission line runs through. You received it from the people who came before. You are passing it to the people who come after. That is not a burden added to the enough. It is a dimension of what the enough actually is.

You are not just here. You are the crossing point of everything that made here possible and everything that here is forming. That is not smaller than enough. It is enormous. And it is still, always, enough.

The wheel turns. The crossing point holds. The carrying continues.

Come back here whenever you need to. Not to finish. Just to remember where you are standing, and what you are standing on, and what you are forming.

Here Is Enough.
