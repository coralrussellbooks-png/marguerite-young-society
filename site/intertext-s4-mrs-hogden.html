Definition from research

Mrs. Hogden, hired by Vera's mother as a companion, carries schoolroom culture and a comic pedigree. She and Catherine needed no interview, "for they had both understood so well that truth is beauty and beauty is truth" (chapter 70). She begins arithmetic with "If Farmer Brown had four apples." She is "a descendant of Daniel Boone and William the Conquerer and Alexander Graham Bell." With Esther and Mr. Bonebreaker, she is one more American claiming a borrowed lineage.
