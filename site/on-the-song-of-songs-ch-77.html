On the Song of Songs

Young calls Esther’s condition the “Song of Songs of the sterility which is the creation.” The Song of Songs (or Song of Solomon) is the Hebrew Bible’s erotic poem: unique in the scriptural canon for celebrating sexual love between equals, two bodies in mutual desire and delight. Commentators across centuries have allegorized it (as God’s love for Israel, as Christ’s love for the Church) but its literal text is about two people reaching toward each other. Young inverts the form: the song of sterility, the poem of what reaches toward creation and finds absence. Esther is the great erotic poem of loss. Her body is the text.
