Intertextuality in MMMD

4. The Drowned Guide

← Previous · Intertextuality in MMMD Home · Next →

Characters: Joachim Spitzer · Miss MacIntosh · Vera Cartwheel

Philosophy: Marguerite Young on Her Craft

Allusions: The Bible

Definition from research

Every guidebook in the novel fails, and Young makes the failure physical.

Miss MacIntosh carries Pilgrim's Progress as her guide, preferring it to the Bible because "old Bunyan was fairly safe even though not altogether trustworthy" (chapter 3). She trusts the Bible less. "The King James version of the Bible was not to be trusted" (chapter 21), and later, "The Bible exaggerates" (chapter 63). Her other guides are almanacs: Franklin's maxims, the Farmer's Almanac, the World Almanac. Vera learns "everything that old Benjamin Franklin had ever said about early to bed and early to rise" (chapter 15).

The guides do not survive contact with the sea. Vera finds the Bunyan "water-logged with marks of sea shells on its pages" (chapter 3). Years later she still argues about it with Mr. Spitzer: "her little Pilgrim's Progress is swollen with salt water" (chapter 68). Franklin's authority collapses through his own inventions. Miss MacIntosh cannot forgive the man who preached early rising and then brought electric light: "it was he who had written that early to bed makes us wise" (chapter 23).

The most striking failure is spoken by the guide herself. In chapter 63, urging Vera forward, Miss MacIntosh says, "ours is a Pilgrim's Progress from nowhere to nowhere." She keeps Bunyan's title and his image of the pilgrim on the road. She removes both ends of the road. There is no City of Destruction to flee and no Celestial City to reach. There is only the walking.

The guides are not mocked. Miss MacIntosh walks on "as if we knew where we were walking," and the novel honors her for it. What fails is the promise of arrival. This strategy carries the principle the notes call "Unfinished Is Honest," and it may be the clearest single sign of what Young wanted her novel to do. The book keeps the old maps, lets the water in, and walks anyway.
