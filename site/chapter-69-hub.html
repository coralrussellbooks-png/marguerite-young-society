Chapter 69: The Room Marked Fire Escape

← Chapter 68 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 70 →

Story arc: Arc 7 The Tavern

Length: Medium, about 0.8% of the novel (5,158 words)

Character hubs: Vera Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh · Catherine Cartwheel · Esther Longtree · Moses Hunnecker · Justice O’Leary · Mr. Weed · Imadaisy · Sarah O’Leary · The Tavern Landlady · The Tavern Landlord · Tillie

Summary

Vera's room is marked Fire Escape. It has a corn-husk mattress, a "Forget me not" picture of a church steeple, and a slop jar holding someone else's hair combings. The landlord points out the other guests on the way: Moses, a knitting girl named Tillie, an old woman with enormous canaries, and Mr. Weed's shadow. All night, through the thin wall, old Dr. Justice O'Leary rages at Sarah, his dead sister, lists every disease he knows, and mentions his strangest case, a woman who is always pregnant. Vera finally screams at Sarah to go.
