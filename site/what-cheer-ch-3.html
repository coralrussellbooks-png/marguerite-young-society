What Cheer (Ch 3)

On Chapter 3 of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965). Part of A Guide to Reading Miss MacIntosh, My Darling.
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

From the reading guide, Chapter 3 · Research: Chapter 3

From the guide

What Cheer, Iowa is a real place. A small town in Keokuk County, Iowa, population around 600. Young maintained in interviews that she had no idea it existed when she invented it as Miss MacIntosh’s hometown. Whether this is entirely true or a charming piece of authorial mythology, the name is perfect: it carries the promise of comfort alongside a slight edge of irony, which is exactly Miss MacIntosh’s register. She offers what cheer she can. It was never going to be enough.
