The Brooklyn Bridge (Ch 4)

On Chapter 4 of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965). Part of A Guide to Reading Miss MacIntosh, My Darling.
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

From the reading guide, Chapter 4 · Research: Chapter 4

From the guide

The Brooklyn Bridge opened in 1883 and immediately became one of the most famous structures in the world and one of the most frequented sites for suicide in New York City. In the era Young is writing about, jumping from the bridge carried a particular meaning for women: it was a public, visible act of refusal. The woman in the wedding veil is refusing something specific. Young does not tell us what. She leaves that to us.
