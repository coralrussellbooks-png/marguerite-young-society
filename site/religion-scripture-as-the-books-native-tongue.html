Definition from research

The first thing the tally makes plain is scale. Religion is not one theme among many in the novel; it is the language the book is written in. Nearly every page reaches for scripture, from Genesis to Revelation, and where the Bible runs out Young reaches further, to Islam and the Talmud, to Buddhism and Ubuntu, to the Greek underworld. Her characters think in psalms and parables because that is the only vocabulary wide enough for what they are asking. When they meet death, love, guilt, or the plain question of what is real, they reach for the sacred stories, because those are the stories a culture keeps for exactly these moments. The book's religiosity is not piety. It is the sound of people using the largest words they have.
