Read-Along 2028

Start Here · Miss MacIntosh, My Darling

In 2028 we read Miss MacIntosh, My Darling together, a few chapters a week, through the whole year. The schedule below runs from the first Wednesday in January to the end of December.

How it works

  • Each week has a reading assignment by chapter, so it works with any edition of the novel.
  • A new post goes out on Substack every Wednesday. Discussion for each week takes place in the comments of that week’s post.
  • Each chapter number links to its Chapter Hub, with a summary, the chapter’s length, and everything on the site about it.
  • The Load column tells you how much reading the week holds. Light weeks are a good time to catch up.
  • Weeks with difficult material carry a content note, and weeks with a recorded reading are marked so you can listen along.

Before you begin

  • Start Here: the front door to every study book on the novel
  • The Story in Time: how the novel loops. The diagram is safe to look at now; save the timeline table for later.
  • Glossary: Young’s Latin and her unusual words
  • Audio: recorded readings of the novel
