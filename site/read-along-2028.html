Read-Along 2028–2030

Start Here · Miss MacIntosh, My Darling

The joy of reading Miss MacIntosh, My Darling isn’t getting to the end; it is living inside the dream.

This is a slow read. One chapter at a time, with no chapters grouped together, from Monday, January 3, 2028 to Sunday, April 28, 2030. Short chapters get one week, longer chapters two, and the longest three. 50 chapters take one week, 26 take two, and 6 take three.

How it works

  • Each chapter has its own post on Substack, 82 in all, going up on the Monday the chapter begins. Discussion takes place in the comments of that post for as long as the chapter runs.
  • Assignments are by chapter, so the plan works with any edition.
  • Each chapter number links to its Chapter Hub, the group’s home base, with links to that chapter in every companion book, its For the Curious entries, and the essays that discuss it.
  • In a two- or three-week chapter, read at whatever pace suits you and use the extra time for the site.

How the site fits each chapter

  • Read the chapter first. The hub summary and the Reading Guide both tell what happens, so they work best as a check afterward.
  • Reading Guide: read “What actually happens,” “What to hold onto,” and “What to watch for as you keep reading.”
  • For the Curious: each chapter has between one and eight entries. The schedule names two to start with, and anyone can pick another to bring to discussion.
  • To All My Darlings research notes: use these for the key passages, symbols, and themes.
  • Life, Love, and Truth: each chapter page ends with “For Reflection” questions, which can serve as discussion prompts.
  • One lens per chapter, rotating: The Eleven Pillars, Woman in Young’s Magnum Opus, Religion, the Intertextual Index, or Comedy. Comedy pages exist for 71 chapters, and the hub lists them when they exist.
  • Site search and “Find on this page” follow one motif across the book, such as butterflies, umbrellas, canaries, wigs, or rain.
  • Story Arc pages, Character Hubs, and Masterclass portraits cover the whole novel. First-time readers should read only the arc Overview when an arc begins and save the rest until the arc ends.
  • With every seventh chapter the group reads one Pillar Crosswalk, eleven in all. At the end of most arcs the group reads a chapter of Exploring Reality and Illusion.
  • Chapter 18 and Chapter 74 deal with sexual violence and carry a content note.
