Definition from research

Bonebreaker's promises to Miss MacIntosh, "Balm in Gilead and myrrh and incense," and "a pillar of clouds by day, a pillar of fire by night," heap up the healing balm of Jeremiah, the temple's incense, and the Exodus pillars that led Israel, a grand offer the book calls a lie.
