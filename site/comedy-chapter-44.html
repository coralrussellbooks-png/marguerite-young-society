Definition from research

The rivalry of the Spitzer brothers is comic in its opposition, the cautious Mr. Spitzer against the flamboyant Peron, who mocks his brother's meticulous order and takes every chance the timid one avoids. Peron is the strutting fool, the "Judas cock" who crows at dawn, a worldly gambler and spender against a man who claims never to have placed a bet. The image of Peron as a preening rooster, all noise and swagger beside his fretful, careful twin, gives the pair the shape of a comic duo, the reckless brother and the one who lives in fear of recklessness.
