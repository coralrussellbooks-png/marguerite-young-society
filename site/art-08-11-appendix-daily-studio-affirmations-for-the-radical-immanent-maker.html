Appendix: Daily Studio Affirmations for the Radical Immanent Maker

← Chapter 9: The Turning of the Stand Shifting from the market · Art When Here Is Enough · The Momentum of the Unheld Gesture →

Pin these above your bench, your welding station, or your wall of tools. Read them out loud when the noise of the market starts to flatten what you see.

My contradictions are my texture. I am not a brand. I am a living, plural maker.

I will not trade a true, frightening complexity for a simpler story just to please a gallery, a feed, or a critic.

I will stay with the mass when it gets uncomfortable. I will witness it. I will not rush to grind away what honestly broke.

The work is unfinished. The material has not set. That is not a failure. That is my lifelong work.

There is no final sealed monument. The market floor is gone, and I am standing at the crossing point.

Make it anyway.
