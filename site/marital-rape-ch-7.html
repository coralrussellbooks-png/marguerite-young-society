Marital rape (Ch 7)

From the reading guide, Chapter 7 · Research: Chapter 7

From the guide

Marital rape was not criminalized in the United States until 1978, when Oregon became the first state to make it illegal. By 1993, all fifty states had laws against it, though many states still treated it more leniently than other forms of rape, and some continue to do so. Young was writing in a world where this violence was entirely legal, entirely invisible, and largely undiscussed. Musidora’s story of being sold to an old man and bearing too many children is a story about this world, told quietly and without editorial comment because none was needed.
