How to Use This Workbook

← Here Is Enough: A Workbook in Radical Immanence · Radical Immanence Collection · Part One: The Ten Ideas →

This workbook gathers the complete body of Here Is Enough material into one place: the philosophy in plain language, responses to the questions people ask most, weekly reflection prompts, short meditations, long-form daily teachings, twelve pillar essays, compressed practices, and seven complete seven-day sequences.

There is no correct order. You can move through it front to back, open it anywhere, return to the same page many times, or skip entire sections because they are not what you need right now. All of that is fine.

The journal lines throughout are not assignments. They are space. Use them or do not. The reflection questions are not tests. They are invitations to look at what is already here before you move on to the next thing.

The practices here are not techniques for self-improvement. They are invitations to honest attention. To stay in the room a little longer than feels comfortable. To witness rather than fix. To be present to the life that is actually happening rather than the one you are waiting for.

You do not have to be ready. You do not have to be further along. You are already in the right place, which is here.

Here Is Enough.
