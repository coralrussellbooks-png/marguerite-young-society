Chapter 55

On Chapter 55 of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965). Part of Christianity and Religion in Miss MacIntosh, My Darling.

No Christian references appear in this chapter.

“as if it were a sea of glass which had no end”: “And before the throne there was a sea of glass like unto crystal” (Revelation 4:6, KJV). See The Bible. Earlier in Chapters 13 and 22.

“the seraph with his wings folded across his eyes”: “Above it stood the seraphims: each one had six wings; with twain he covered his face, and with twain he covered his feet, and with twain he did fly” (Isaiah 6:2, KJV). See The Bible. Earlier in Chapter 3.

“two pale cavaliers riding two pale horses”: “And I looked, and behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him” (Revelation 6:8, KJV). See The Bible. Earlier in Chapters 9 and 53.

“For what was time?”: Augustine asked the same question: “What then is time? If no one asks me, I know: if I wish to explain it to one that asketh, I know not” (Augustine, Confessions 11.14, trans. E. B. Pusey, 1838). See Augustine and Christian Theology.
