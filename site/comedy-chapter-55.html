Characters: Joachim Spitzer · Peron Spitzer

Definition from research

The twins’ house of mirrored rooms is furnished with comic pairs: twin portraits of Hoboken ferry boats, twin polo players, twin batting averages and twin baseballs, all Peron’s. Joachim’s side of the inventory is cancelled checks, obsolete bank balances, theatre tickets he forgot to use, and invitations he forgot to answer or answered years later.

Best of all are the letters: one from a dead lady asking him to come and hear the virginal, another from a dead lady asking him to come to tea “and bring his brother, the musician,” mistaking which twin was which. Peron’s motto was seize the day and Joachim’s was this is the labor, and the labor, it turns out, was paying for Peron’s day: the forged checks, the debts, the funeral, and the bankruptcy that leaves Joachim in a beachcomber’s shack.
