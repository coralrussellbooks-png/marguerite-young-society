Definition from research

Cousin Hannah is a suffrage captain who travels to Mecca, Medinah, and Fez and speaks of her campaigns as Crusades. Young calls her life "a history of skirts." Her crusading has two faces. Her fight is real, and she sees the rules of gender clearly because she stands outside them, which is "The Margins Reveal the Truth." But her borrowed language of holy war is also the grand destiny rhetoric the philosophy distrusts. Young lets both be true. Notably, Hannah "scorned personal immortality," and seems to have felt this mortal life was the only one, which quietly places her closer to "The Sacred Is Here" than her crusader imagery suggests.
