Brief Overview of Radical Immanence

← The Wheel That Turns Around Nothing · Teaching a Writing Masterclass · The Plot →

You are not one self.

Reality is constructed.

Grief doesn't end.

Witnessing is the core ethical act.

Build anyway, knowing it's temporary.

Unfinished is honest.

The margins reveal the truth.

The past doesn't go away.

Language shapes what's possible.

The sacred is here.

The crossing point is the place where a person stands, the exact here-and-now of a life.

For the writer: I am not suggesting that you need to develop an entire philosophical system to embed in your writing and I am also saying your writing needs to say something and/or be based on something. It does not need to be traceable to some school of thought. You can also use your imagination.

The Marguerite Young Society is dedicated to the work of Marguerite Young (1908 to 1995).
