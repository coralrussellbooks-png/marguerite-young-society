Intertextual Index Home

Character: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Butterflies with mythic names (Aphrodite, Apollo, Io, Adonis blues), a litany of the names of God, Charon's coin, Styx and Hades, Inferno and Paradiso, twin portraits of Washington, Monroe, Garfield and Isabella, a "Decameron of sports" learned from his brother, Haydn, Strauss and the Fiacre Song, Blackstone.

Pattern

His references come doubled and come as afterlife geography. He carries the ferryman's fare in his purse, imagines the Divine Comedy as an escalator, and cannot tell one imaginary Napoleon from another. Everything in his brother's house hangs in pairs. His catalogs are naturalist's catalogs: myth survives in him as the Latin names of moths.

Key references (chapters)

Butterflies (29); names of God (29); Divine Comedy (29), Inferno and Paradiso (54); Charon (46, 51, 53, 59); twin portraits (55); music (62); Cleopatra case (26).

Line from the novel

"God was many things to many people." (ch. 29)
