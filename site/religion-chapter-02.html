Allusions: Genesis

Definition from research

"Our Heavenly Father" opens the Lord's Prayer, the prayer Jesus taught his followers.

"Jacob's ladder to heaven" recalls Genesis 28, Jacob's dream of a ladder joining heaven and earth with angels moving up and down it, a sign of God's presence and promise.

"decapitated, bleeding head of John the Baptist" points to the wilderness prophet who baptized Jesus and was beheaded by Herod for his blunt preaching against the powerful.

"unbaptized babes and of green leaves in Purgatory" brings in the Catholic middle state, the limbo of infants who died unbaptized, "a temporary state forever in transition," souls held between salvation and loss.
