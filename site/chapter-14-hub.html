Chapter 14: The Black Coachman

← Chapter 13 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 15 →

Story arc: Arc 2 The Birthday Night and Its Aftermath

Length: Very long, about 2.3% of the novel (15,216 words)

Character hubs: Vera Cartwheel · Catherine Cartwheel · Joachim Spitzer · Peron Spitzer · The Black Coachman · Mr. Chandelier · Mr. Res Tacamah · The Old Clam Digger · The Sea-House Servants

Summary

Vera comes to the grave of the black coachman, who once drove her grandfather's carriage with four white horses. Catherine is obsessed with that grave and its location. She questions Mr. Spitzer endlessly about the coachman's burial and the fate of the horses. Beneath this obsession, the story of the Spitzer twins comes out. Peron fell from a tall building, probably by suicide. Joachim has lived in the aftermath ever since, wearing his brother's cufflinks and being mistaken for him in the street. Catherine goes on talking long after Mr. Spitzer has left, asking about the grave of the other Mr. Spitzer, the one she loved, until dawn puts her to sleep.
