Characters: Madge Capehorn

Allusions: Shakespeare

Definition from research

The townspeople of chapter 6 carry small-town mythmaking. Their favorite girl was Juliet in a school play: "She was Juliet, and I was Romeo." She was also "Abraham Lincoln's sweetheart and Lincoln's mother afterwards and Jenny Lind." One of them explains the process plainly: "We elected her to be immortal."

The heritage arrives misremembered and still loved. Madge insists that another girl was "no Venus Adonis, no handsome girl with a broken arm. She was no Helen of Troy, no face to launch a thousand ships" (chapter 6). The fused Venus and Adonis, with the Venus de Milo's broken arm, is the town's own myth.
