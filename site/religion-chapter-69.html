Allusions: Genesis

Definition from research

The landlord's skin is "as many colors as Joseph's coat," the coat of Genesis.

Dr. O'Leary cries "My God! Why hast thou deserted me?" and "Jerusalem, Jerusalem," Christ's forsakenness on the cross joined to Jesus's lament over the city.
