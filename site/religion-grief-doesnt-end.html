Grief Doesn't End

See also: Pillar 3 Grief Doesn't End

Characters: Cousin Hannah · Esther Longtree · Joachim Spitzer

Themes: Grief, Loss, and Mourning

Definition from research

No book takes more seriously the radical immanence claim that grief does not resolve but accumulates. Esther Longtree is "everlastingly pregnant" and forever bereaved, her stillborn children dropping from her like leaves, a sorrow that does not pass through tidy stages but piles up into a permanent condition of caring in a world where nothing stays. Mr. Spitzer carries an "immortal guilt" and an eternal bereavement for his brother that no ritual discharges. Cousin Hannah's death opens no clean ending, only more mourning, and the novel's recurring equation, that weddings are funerals and funerals weddings, refuses the consolation of a grief that finishes. Loss in Young does not get processed. It becomes the ground.
