Chapter 34: Why No Man Had Been This Woman

Summary

Catherine recalls being married both in white and in black, with a dog reciting the ceremony in Latin and a St. Bernard giving her the last sacrament. Then she remembers Hannah's presence in her room: iron keys at her belt, a metallic energy, sharp wit, contempt for mortal men, and total indifference to Mr. Spitzer. In Hannah's company Catherine felt both flirtatious and guilty, sensing a love she could not name. Hannah always predicted that Joachim would fail Catherine at the crucial moment, as Peron had by his suicide.
