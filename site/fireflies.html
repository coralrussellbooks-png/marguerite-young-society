Definition from research

Chapter 3 · Fireflies in a Jar

  • Symbolize hope and guidance in darkness.
  • The moment when Miss MacIntosh uses them as a lamp suggests her role as a beacon of reason amid confusion.

Chapter 43 · Firefly Light / Star

  • Symbolizes hope, guidance, and the fragile spark of life amid darkness.

Chapter 45 · Fireflies and Stars

  • Fleeting Beauty, lost souls, and unreachable ideals.

Chapter 49 · Birds (Pigeons, Blackbirds, Fireflies)

  • Birds symbolize life, freedom, and the passage of souls.
  • The extinction of the passenger pigeon is a metaphor for loss and the fragility of existence.
  • Fireflies and moths symbolize faint, fragile light, hope, memory, or the soul’s flicker.

Chapter 50 · Fireflies and Light

  • Recurring symbols of the soul or consciousness, fragile and transient, yet illuminating the darkness.

Chapter 62 · The Firefly

  • Symbolizes fragile life and the fleeting nature of existence and memory.

Chapter 72 · Fireflies, moths, and butterflies

  • Represent fleeting life, transformation, and the ethereal nature of existence.
