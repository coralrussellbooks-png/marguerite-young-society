Pillar: Pillar 1 Not One Self · Across every book

Where did you try to be one person today, and what did it cost you?

The hunger is to be single and consistent, with the contradictions ironed out. Power offers one clean identity in exchange for the noise inside you. The plural self refuses the trade. You are many, and the contradiction in you is not a defect. It is what a person is.

Notice

For one day, catch the moments you feel pressure to be consistent. This might be smoothing over an opinion so it matches what you said yesterday, or hiding a want because it does not fit the version of you that other people expect.

Write down two or three moments you noticed this pressure today.

Practice

List the different people you are. The version of you at work, the version with your parents, the version alone at eleven at night, the version with an old friend. Do not try to reconcile them. Just name them, side by side.

Reflection

Where did you feel most tired today trying to hold yourself together as one person?

What happened in your body when you let two contradictory things be true about yourself at once, even for a moment?
