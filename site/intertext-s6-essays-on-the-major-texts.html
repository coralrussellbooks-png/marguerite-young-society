Definition from research

The essays below take the major source texts one at a time. Each opens by naming its category, obligatory, optional, or accidental, so a reader knows from the start how firmly that text is anchored in the novel. Pilgrim's Progress comes first because it carries the most structural weight. The texts Young names or quotes outright follow, then the texts she evokes without citing by title, and last the connections that belong to readers.
