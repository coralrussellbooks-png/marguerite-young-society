On dream logic and the logic of the opposite.

The folk tradition that dreams mean the opposite of what they show (dreaming of death means long life, dreaming of money means loss) predates Freud by millennia. Freud formalized it as the distinction between manifest and latent content, with the dream's censor disguising the true meaning in reversed or condensed form. Young gives Mr. Spitzer conscious access to this logic as a navigation tool, and it still fails him, because the tailor's shop is not accessible through any navigational method: direct or reversed. It is the shop that exists in the debt relationship between a person and their grief, and no city directory has ever catalogued that address. You cannot find it. It finds you. It sends bills.
