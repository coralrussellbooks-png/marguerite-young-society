Marguerite Young’s own sexuality (Ch 9)

From the reading guide, Chapter 9 · Research: Chapter 9

From the guide

Marguerite Young’s own sexuality is worth noting here. Young, who never married, had relationships with both men and women, she described a relationship in which she told a man he could visit her every Tuesday for the rest of his life but she would not marry him. When the word “gay” appears in Chapter 9, it may carry this awareness. Young was writing in an era when the word was shifting from its older meaning of carefree happiness toward its newer one, and she was writing from inside a life that would not have been entirely legible to the world she was depicting. The novel is, among its many other things, a book written by a woman who understood what it meant to live outside the expected scripts.
