Characters: Catherine Cartwheel

Philosophy: Augustine and Christian Theology

Allusions: Ancient Egypt · Genesis

Definition from research

A gardener believes himself the spouse of Potiphar, the Egyptian captain, recalling Genesis 39, Potiphar's wife and the falsely accused Joseph.

Catherine keeps trying to telephone the Bishop of Hippo, who is always out, a dreamlike call placed to St. Augustine himself.
