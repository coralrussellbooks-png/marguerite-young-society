Alexander Pope (Ch 34)

From the reading guide, Chapter 34 · Research: Chapter 34

From the guide

Alexander Pope, who appears holding Catherine’s hand during the wedding, was the eighteenth-century English poet most famous for his Essay on Man and his translations of Homer. He was also famously small and physically deformed, brilliant and acerbic and deeply interested in the question of what it means to be human in a universe governed by forces beyond human understanding. Young places him at Catherine’s wedding without comment. He is there because weddings are, in Young’s world, occasions that raise the Essay on Man question directly: what is this creature, and who designed the system he is caught in?
