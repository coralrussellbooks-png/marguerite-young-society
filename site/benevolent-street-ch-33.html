Benevolent Street (Ch 33)

From the reading guide, Chapter 33 · Research: Chapter 33

From the guide

Benevolent Street is a real street in Providence, Rhode Island, not Boston, which may be Young’s private joke, or may be her deliberate blurring of New England geography. Either way, the name is precise for what it carries: the street of benevolence, where Peron is reportedly seen peering into doorways, the same street Mr. Spitzer walks the next evening in his dark cape. The benevolence is Mr. Spitzer’s, and it costs him everything.
