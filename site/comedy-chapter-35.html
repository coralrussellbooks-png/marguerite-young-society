Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

The comedy of mistaken identity clings to Mr. Spitzer, who keeps being greeted as his own dead brother and has to explain, with mounting embarrassment, that he is "not his dead brother" but a perfect stranger who happens to share the face. His fumbling, over-careful protests, delivered to callers who will not believe he is himself, make him a figure of gentle absurdity, a man forever proving his own existence and losing the argument. His pose as a kind of cartographer of dreams, charting the mother's visions on a mariner's chart of waters no one has sailed, adds to the picture of a lawyer permanently lost in someone else's map.
