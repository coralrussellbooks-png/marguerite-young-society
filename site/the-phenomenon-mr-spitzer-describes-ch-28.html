The phenomenon Mr. Spitzer describes (Ch 28)

From the reading guide, Chapter 28 · Research: Chapter 28

From the guide

The phenomenon Mr. Spitzer describes (where “the butterfly perching upon the flower” becomes identical to the flower, twin to twin, until the flower flies away and the winged petals remain) is a real observational puzzle that has fascinated naturalists. Certain species of butterfly achieve near-perfect mimicry of the flowers they rest on. Young takes this biological fact and makes it Mr. Spitzer’s founding mystery: the creature and its world so perfectly matched that you cannot tell them apart, until one of them moves.
