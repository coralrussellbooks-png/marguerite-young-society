Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

Mr. Spitzer keeps an orthodoxy of his own making. He lives by strict private ritual, punctual and exact, his manner and his rooms carrying a churchlike solemnity, and he sets his table with salt shakers, crystal, and gold plate as if expecting the divine to arrive for dinner. The discipline is real, but the belief underneath it is anything but settled.

He is a skeptic who cannot quite let go of hope. He holds faith and doubt at once, sits easily with paradox, and is always aware of how little the human mind can grasp of God. A musician, he thinks of the divine as a silent, perfect harmony beyond all earthly discord, and his unplayed compositions stand for a peace he reaches for and never quite hears. He is unsure of the afterlife, unsure at times of his own identity, forever shadowed by his dead brother and the question of which of them he is.

For all his doubt, he serves. He acts as executor for the dead, companion to the dying, and searcher after lost souls and lost heirs, and that steady care amounts to a kind of faith even when he can promise no cosmic reward for it. He neither embraces doctrine nor rejects the possibility of the sacred. He simply keeps watch at the border between them.
