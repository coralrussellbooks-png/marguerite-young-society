On Mata Hari and the Spy Mother

The little toy salesman’s mother is described as a world-famous beauty and spy: the Mata Hari type. Mata Hari (born Margaretha Zelle, 1876–1917) was a Dutch exotic dancer who became a World War I spy–or who was accused of being one; the evidence was always disputed–and was executed by the French. She became the archetype of the beautiful female spy, her identity so theatrical that it was unclear where the performance ended and the person began. The little toy salesman’s mother belongs to this tradition: a woman whose life was the performance of a character, who left her son a bottle baby while she lived her larger story. He inherited her unreachability.
