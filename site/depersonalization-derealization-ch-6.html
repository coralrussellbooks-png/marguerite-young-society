Depersonalization-derealization (Ch 6)

From the reading guide, Chapter 6 · Research: Chapter 6

From the guide

Depersonalization-derealization is the psychological term for what Young seems to be describing through Madge in this chapter, a dissociative state in which a person feels detached from their own body and mind, as if watching themselves from outside, or as if the world around them is unreal. It can be triggered by trauma, including sexual violence. Young, writing in the 1940s, did not have this vocabulary. But she renders the experience with remarkable precision.
