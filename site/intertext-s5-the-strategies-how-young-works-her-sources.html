Definition from research

The index shows that certain moves recur no matter which character holds the reference. A bus-driver and a Boston heiress, a nursemaid and a lawyer, all handle their inherited texts in a few repeated ways. The essays below name nine of those ways. Chapter numbers refer to the novel. Every quotation comes from the novel's text.
