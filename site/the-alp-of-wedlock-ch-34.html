The Alp of wedlock (Ch 34)

From the reading guide, Chapter 34 · Research: Chapter 34

From the guide

The Alp of wedlock (Hannah’s phrase, or Catherine’s version of it) is Young’s most concentrated metaphor for marriage as an impassable peak. Hannah had climbed the Alps. She had been jealous of Mont Blanc, of the Jungfrau, the Eiger, Monte Rosa. She had waited “for snow-peaked mountains to reach sea level.” But the Alp of wedlock, nothing so adamantine, nothing so undissolving. The mountain Hannah never climbed is the one she spent her life at the base of.
