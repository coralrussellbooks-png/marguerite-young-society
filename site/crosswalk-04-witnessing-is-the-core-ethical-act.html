Pillar 4 Witnessing Is The Core Ethical Act

← Pillar 3 · Pillar Crosswalks Home · Pillar 5 →

Every book's treatment of this pillar, side by side.

The central ethical stance is not to fix or conclude, but to stay in the room. To see fully, without flinching, without rushing toward resolution. Attention is the practice. Refusal to simplify is the act.
