On the Name Oliver

Mr. Weed tells Vera that his first wife’s maiden name was Oliver, and that “the Olivers were known for hanging” themselves. Young has stacked this name with history: Andrew Oliver, the Massachusetts official responsible for implementing the Stamp Act, was hanged in effigy. Oliver Cromwell was disinterred two years after his death and posthumously hanged, then beheaded. Oliver Moore was lynched in North Carolina in 1930, his body left on display while thousands gathered. The name is a genealogy of the rope.
