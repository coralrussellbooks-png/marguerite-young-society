The skirt factory Hannah picketed in (Ch 32)

From the reading guide, Chapter 32 · Research: Chapter 32

From the guide

The skirt factory Hannah picketed in a cold sleet rain, forgetting “she owned most of the town and most of the skirts,” is one of Young’s most quietly devastating comic details. Cousin Hannah, the great suffragette who had campaigned on every front for woman’s liberation, picketed her own holdings without noticing. Young places this without comment. The chapter has been building Hannah’s complexity for two chapters now, and this detail is the capstone: she was fighting against something she was also sustaining. The most committed activists are not exempt from contradiction.
