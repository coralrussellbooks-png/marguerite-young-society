Pillar: Pillar 3 Grief Doesn't End · Across every book

What loss have you been waiting to see paid back, rather than letting it simply stay?

The hunger is to be done with a loss, to have it explained, redeemed, or avenged, so you can be whole again. Power offers your grief a destination and a face. This pillar lets grief be weather, something you live inside rather than something you resolve. Carried without needing it paid back, it cannot be aimed at anyone.

Notice

Name one grief you carry that has not closed, and probably will not. It can be small or large. Notice, without judging it, whether any part of you has assigned it a cause or a culprit.

Practice

Write a letter to that grief. Do not ask it to leave and do not aim it at anyone. Describe it the way you would describe weather, something present, something that changes day to day, something you are learning to live under rather than something owed to you.

Reflection

Who or what have you wanted to blame for a grief that never found a proper target?

What changes in you when you set down the need for it to be avenged, even without the grief itself going away?
