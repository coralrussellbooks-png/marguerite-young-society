The thimble engraved with “Juliet, 1899” (Ch 12)

From the reading guide, Chapter 12 · Research: Chapter 12

From the guide

The thimble engraved with “Juliet, 1899” is one of the novel’s small mysteries. Young never explains it. Juliet is the name of a woman who died for love. 1899 is the year before a new century. The thimble is used to tap children back to their senses. Draw your own conclusions, but don’t expect resolution.
