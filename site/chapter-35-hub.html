Chapter 35: She Had Been Married to the Suffrage Movement

← Chapter 34 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 36 →

Story arc: Arc 4 Cousin Hannah

Length: Medium, about 0.9% of the novel (6,147 words)

Character hubs: Vera Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh · Catherine Cartwheel · Joachim Spitzer · Cousin Hannah

Summary

Catherine imagines inviting Hannah back for a winter of card games. She reviews Hannah's suffrage career on every front, her conquests and her refusal of any memorial window, and all the things Hannah was not: no dancer, no hostess, no keeper of private confessions. She concludes that Hannah was married to the suffrage movement as if it were her husband. Mr. Spitzer notes Catherine's habit of always taking the opposite view.
