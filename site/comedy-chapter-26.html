Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

Mr. Spitzer’s easiest case was an elderly Boston lady who believed she was the reincarnation of Cleopatra, though there had been no Marc Antony in her past, and who lived next door to a rival spinster who believed exactly the same thing. To taunt the neighbor’s Sphinx, she built a rival Sphinx in her garden among the tiger lilies, and a greater pyramid than her neighbor could afford.

She left her house and fortune to her innumerable Siamese cats, with the provision that the old caretaker could live there on an income for as long as the cats lived. Mr. Spitzer, very cautious and very conservative, has administered the estate so well that it has not diminished, and the cats seem likely to live forever. The legal mind does its careful duty by an absurdity, and the absurdity outlasts everyone.
