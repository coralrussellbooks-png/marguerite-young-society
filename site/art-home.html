Art When Here Is Enough

Radical Immanence · The Marguerite Young Society

This book was written before the philosophy reached its final form, so some pillars here carry earlier names or numbers. The Eleven Pillars page shows the complete, final version.

How Radical Immanence lives in other art forms, an invitation to make work from it, and seven studio manuals for writers, painters, sculptors, musicians, dancers, actors and fiber artists.

A Song Inspired by Miss MacIntosh, My Darling

Andrew Bunney wrote “Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (The Heart of the Rose)” in 1998, building the song from images in Marguerite Young’s novel. It appeared on his album MAYDAY in 2000. The lyric video pairs it with Super 8 film shot in the UK in 1977 and Mini DV footage from Port Willunga, Australia, in 2001.

Musicians: Jon Van Bowman (guitar), Steve Fleming (bass), Steve Shier (drums), Tristan Andrews (organ) and Adam Hollinshead (cello), with backing vocals by Helen Ellis, Alex Frost and Louise Niva. Produced by Jon Van Bowman and Nick Burton. Film by Puppet Show Films, with Dr Hel Carter as co-cinematographer and co-editor. Watch on YouTube
