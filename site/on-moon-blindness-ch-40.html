On moon blindness.

Catherine claims to suffer from moon blindness, though she knows perfectly well it is a condition that afflicts horses, not humans. Moon blindness, equine recurrent uveitis, causes progressive vision loss in horses through inflammation of the eye, its cause unknown. Catherine claims it anyway. She has always been, in Young's phrase from earlier chapters, beautifully obtuse about the difference between her condition and the conditions of other creatures. She is a person for whom the categories of human and animal, like the categories of living and dead, are less fixed than convention requires. If horses go moon-blind, she sees no reason she should not.
