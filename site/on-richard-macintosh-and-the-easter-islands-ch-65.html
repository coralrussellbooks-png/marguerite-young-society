From the guide

On Richard MacIntosh and the Easter Islands.

Miss MacIntosh's brother, Richard, “a primitive Christian bishop from Iowa,” went to convert the heathen and disappeared, presumably taken by headhunters. Miss MacIntosh carries this loss too, the absent brother who went to the end of the world to give people something they hadn't asked for, and didn't come back. Young places this alongside Mr. Bonebreaker's failed mission to save Miss MacIntosh's soul: both men wanted to convert someone. Neither succeeded. Both vanished from her life.
