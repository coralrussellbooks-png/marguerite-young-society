An Extension of Here Is Enough

A Workbook in Radical Immanence

Here Is Enough asked you to look at the present moment. To be in the life that is already happening. To stop deferring.

The Crossing Point asks you to look at where you are standing. Not just what is here, but what here is made of. The weight pressing up from below. The ground you are forming for the people who come after.

You are not just a person moving through time. You are the place where time crosses itself. The transmission line runs through you. It ran through everyone who made you possible. It will run through everyone you make possible. Here is still enough. And here is also enormous.
