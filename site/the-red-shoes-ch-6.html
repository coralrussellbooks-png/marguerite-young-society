The Red Shoes (Ch 6)

From the reading guide, Chapter 6 · Research: Chapter 6

From the guide

“The Red Shoes” is a fairy tale by Hans Christian Andersen in which a vain girl is condemned to dance forever in enchanted shoes, unable to stop even after her death. Young uses this image in reference to Madge: a woman compelled to keep moving through a life she did not choose, unable to stop, the motion itself become a kind of punishment. The red shoes are not freedom. They never were.
