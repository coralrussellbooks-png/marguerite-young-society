Characters: Esther Longtree

Definition from research

"her own was the virgin birth," Esther says, "but she was Jesus Christ or anybody holy," and yet "popular and vulgar... dropping stillborn," the sacred claim collapsing into her earthy, grieving life.

"A dove of God... impregnated Mary," so "Christ was born and fatherless in the barnyard," retells the Annunciation, the Spirit as a dove, in her own rough, homely terms.
