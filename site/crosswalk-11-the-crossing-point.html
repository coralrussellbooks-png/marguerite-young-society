Pillar 11 The Crossing Point

← Pillar 10 · Pillar Crosswalks Home

Every book's treatment of this pillar, side by side.

You are not a self moving through time. You are where time crosses itself. You are constituted by what was deposited before you arrived, and you are constituting the ground the not-yet-born will stand on. This obligation is not chosen. It is structural. The ancestors are in the sediment. The carrying continues.

Vera also says, "I do not know the night in which I conceived as I do not know the night in which I was conceived." She stands exactly at "The Crossing Point," between the generation that made her and the one she carries, with neither origin fully known. Set against Esther Longtree's endless pregnancy that never delivers, Vera's conception is the book's one real opening toward a future.
