Characters: Joachim Spitzer · Cousin Hannah

Definition from research

Mr. Spitzer, as Hannah’s executor, takes her alpenhorn for his collection of instruments and then wanders a fog-bound city asking strangers whether they have seen her. His navigation is comic seamanship on dry land: his cape drags him from starboard to larboard, and he leaves his course uncharted so that he might gain new orientations through disorientation.

Out of the fog lurches an old steeplejack, “tall as a church steeple,” who remembers Hannah and calls her beautiful. Then he answers in paradoxes: he exists and does not exist, he lives because he is dead, he is dead as the census taker and the watchman. Mr. Spitzer has finally found someone who talks the way he thinks, and the encounter is both a comic echo and a mirror.
