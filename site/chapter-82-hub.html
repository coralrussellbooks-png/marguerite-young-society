Chapter 82: The End Is the Beginning

← Chapter 81 · Chapter Hubs Home

Story arc: Arc 9 The Ending

Length: Long, about 2.0% of the novel (13,423 words)

Character hubs: Vera Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh · Catherine Cartwheel · Joachim Spitzer · Peron Spitzer · Esther Longtree · Moses Hunnecker · Justice O’Leary · Madge Capehorn · Mrs. Hogden · Mr. Weed · Jock Cartwheel · The Stone Deaf Man · The Old Clam Digger · The Black Coachman · Lucie Bell

Summary

Vera thinks of all she could have confessed to Esther. Her mother is dead. The sea swept away the house with its golden towers, leaving chandeliers strewn along the shore, and only the string of pearls she wears remains to her. She is mourning three deaths. At the end, Catherine rose from her bed and walked. Mr. Spitzer carried her into the garden by the sea and declared his love. He told her he was really Peron, and that it was Joachim who had died. But Catherine had already died in his arms before he spoke. He also lost his old valet, the clam-digger, in the storm. Vera sees Miss MacIntosh anew: "Whatever is average is the dream. Whatever is hidden is the revealed." At dawn in the hotel corridor she meets a stone deaf man, who lost his hearing at fourteen. They become lovers, and he whispers that their child will be his ear. They plan to marry and invite everyone, the flowers and birds and Esther Longtree, who hangs a misspelled sign in the restaurant window, "Owt to luntsch. bee bak in a whale."
