Cousin Hannah as Carrier

Part of Intertextual Index, a study of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965).
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

Intertextual Index Home

Character: Cousin Hannah

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Saladin, Suleiman, Sardanapalus, Ashurbanipal, the Seleucids, Hannibal, Napoleon, Arabia Deserta, Moab, the Dead Sea, El-Burraq, Al-Sirat, Mohammed, Babylon, Ur, Nineveh, Jason, the Holy Grail, the Black Knight, Tristram or Yseult; a house hung with Custer, Sheridan and Paul Revere.

Pattern

Conquest and quest. Nearly every reference casts her in a male heroic role (Napoleon's crossed arm, the Black Knight, Jason), and the quests fail: she is "that Jason who had not found the golden fleece." Her geography is the East as a campaign theater. Much of it reaches the reader secondhand through Catherine's dreaming, and at her death the conquests return as delirium ("Is that Ur of the Chaldees...").

Key references (chapters)

Sultans and Assyrian kings (30); Arabia Deserta, El-Burraq (30); Al-Sirat (36); Jason, Holy Grail (42); war pictures (42); dying questions (43).

Line from the novel

"She was that Jason who had not found the golden fleece." (ch. 42)
