Tempus fugit (Ch 18)

From the reading guide, Chapter 18 · Research: Chapter 18

From the guide

Tempus fugit, time flies, is the last thing Miss MacIntosh says in the chapter, rocking Vera toward sleep, having just claimed that she is not so old as Vera thinks, that she is no older “than the day she was born.” “Age is the most difficult thing to achieve.” The woman who wound Miss MacIntosh’s alarm clock every day, the woman who denied that time repeats itself, “a day being gone when a day was gone”: ends the chapter with the oldest, most worn acknowledgment that time passes. She has been fighting it all her life. She is still fighting it. She is losing.
