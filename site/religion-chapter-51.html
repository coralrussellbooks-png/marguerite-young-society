Philosophy: Augustine and Christian Theology

Allusions: Angels and Archangels · Genesis

Definition from research

"the hounds of Nimrod barking through clouds" invokes Nimrod, Genesis's "mighty hunter before the Lord."

"the Holy Grail which was the cup of the moon" reaches for the legendary cup of the Last Supper, light shining through it where there is neither sun nor moon.

"like Gabriel blowing his horn" brings back the messenger angel.

The "errors, mistakes, illusions" that "never evolved from an original sin" turn on the Fall, wondering whether the world's flaws come from a sin at all or from a "dreaming or a splitting God."

"cherubim of barnacles sculptured by the sea" makes angels out of the tide's leavings, the sacred shaped by chance.
