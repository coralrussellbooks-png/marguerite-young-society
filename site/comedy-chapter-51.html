Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

Mr. Spitzer has not been seen in court for years, and his practice has a new archivist: a mouse in his desk drawer who has gnawed his leases, mortgages, wills, and affidavits into flakes like snow. He catalogs the clients he might represent instead, and the list drifts happily out of law: lawyer for a dolphin and a rose, lawyer for a starfish, lawyer for a passenger pigeon.

In his wanderings he meets people with illusions as elaborate as his own: an old woman who thinks she is a bird cage from which the bird has flown, an old piano tuner who taps his forehead with a tuning fork and declares it hollow, a blind portrait painter who wants to paint him. Each is a small comic portrait, and each reflects him back.
