Go Tell Aunt Rhody (Ch 5)

From the reading guide, Chapter 5 · Research: Chapter 5

From the guide

“Go Tell Aunt Rhody” has a melody that originated as a minuet in an eighteenth-century French opera and arrived in America through hymnals. The lyrics tell a simple, bleak story: the old gray goose that was being saved to make a feather bed has died in the millpond. Young uses it here as part of the chapter’s sustained meditation on what has died in America and what, if anything, can be made from the feathers.
