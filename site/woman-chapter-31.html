Woman in Young's Magnum Opus

Chapter 31

Other versions: Chapter hub · Research notes · The Wheel and the Spokes

Characters: Catherine Cartwheel · Cousin Hannah · Joachim Spitzer · Peron Spitzer

Cousin Hannah is a woman the record cannot hold, and that failure of the record is the chapter's pillar. Mr. Spitzer's every attempt to reach her, the unanswered doors, the letters, the uncertainty about her name and dates and even her existence, is the erasure of women's histories enacted in real time, the patriarchal archive unable to contain a woman who lived outside its categories. She rides "through deserts and mountains alone, shrouded in purple and gold," beyond the reach of male bureaucracy, and her elusiveness is not an accident but the shape of her autonomy.

Her feminism is stated plainly and lived hard. She is the suffragist who marched "through snow, deserts, and forbidden cities," who confronts "Persian lions, sheiks, African kings, Turks," whose battles are literal and figurative at once. She never married, carried "a great secret," and lived a nomadic life outside marriage and domesticity; her refusal to commemorate Peron's suicide, her indifference to Catherine's lawyer, are refusals of the emotional economies that bind women to men through mourning and memory. This is radical female agency and it is the difficulty of narrating a life the culture has no form for, the same difficulty.

Mr. Spitzer's imagined epitaphs gather the paradox. He honors a warrior "who fought many battles" yet "could not save herself" from "the domestic war" and "mortal frailty," and the balance of public valor and private vulnerability is the whole trouble of a feminist life told in words made by others. His hesitation to finish the epitaph, his legalistic effort to pin her identity, is patriarchal management failing to capture the fullness of a woman who lived past its documentation. Hannah's scorn for "the external man" and "the phantoms of the air," her "oath" against men and rivals, closes the chapter on a woman who has broken with dependency altogether, and the record keeps failing to hold her, which is exactly her victory.
