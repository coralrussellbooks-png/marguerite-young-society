Characters: Cousin Hannah · Catherine Cartwheel

Definition from research

Catherine cannot remember her wedding, so she remembers many. She was married on the tennis court by passing one great ball, which was the moon. She was married on the archery range by shooting a golden arrow through a cloud. She was married to a sailboat that lifted its wings and sailed away, to a porpoise and a star, to an iceberg, to a church bell.

Into this procession charges Cousin Hannah, beating the bridegroom with her white umbrella and screaming like ten thousand demons, trying to carry the bride away like the robber bridegroom of the fairy tale. The suffragette who urged women to fly their skirts as banners against tyrannical man becomes a one-woman riot at a wedding, and the scene is both farce and the first clear sign of how much Hannah loved Catherine.
