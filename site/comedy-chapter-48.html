Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

The comedy of Mr. Spitzer's bafflement deepens as he moves through a ghost-filled city, endlessly rechecking, doubting, and confusing one sense with another, his own ear "roaring" with music only he can hear. The surreal streets are crowded with phantom horses and half-real figures, and Spitzer, obese and over-cautious, is perpetually the man who cannot find his way through a place that keeps rearranging itself. The humor is that of a scrupulous, literal-minded man set loose in a city that obeys dream logic, forever poking with his cane at doorways that turn out to be something else.
