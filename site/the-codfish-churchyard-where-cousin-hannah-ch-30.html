The codfish churchyard where Cousin Hannah (Ch 30)

On Chapter 30 of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965). Part of A Guide to Reading Miss MacIntosh, My Darling.
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

From the reading guide, Chapter 30 · Research: Chapter 30

From the guide

The codfish churchyard where Cousin Hannah is buried, tombs “marked only with the sign of the codfish,” is a real New England tradition. The codfish was the symbol of the Massachusetts fishing industry and the economic foundation of the Puritan settlements. Boston’s Sacred Cod hangs in the State House. To be buried with the codfish sign is to be of the original New England aristocracy: the salt-sea Puritan stock. Cousin Hannah, who went everywhere else in the world, lies forever in the most rooted of New England ground.
