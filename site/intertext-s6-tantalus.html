Definition from research

Category: obligatory. Vera names the myth outright and uses it to describe her life.

In Greek myth Tantalus was punished in the underworld by standing in a pool beneath a fruit tree. Whenever he bent to drink, the water drained away. Whenever he reached for fruit, the branch rose out of reach.

Vera says it plainly in the first chapter: "All my life I had been reaching for the tangible, and it had evaded me, much like the myth of Tantalus, much as if the tangible itself were an illusion." That one sentence is the plot of the novel. Vera spends years searching for Miss MacIntosh, the one solid person in a house of dreams, and every time the real seems close enough to hold, it recedes. Young names very few myths as keys to her own book. This is the one she names first.
