Summary

The twins had opposite mottos: Peron's "carpe diem," seize the day, and Joachim's "hoc opus, hic labor est," this is the work, this the labor. Joachim had planned a quiet life of music and stamp collecting in their twin house of mirrored rooms. Peron borrowed, forged his brother's checks, and left debts. When Peron died, Joachim paid for the funeral and the debts, went bankrupt, and lost the house. He now lives in a beachcomber's shack, still wondering whose funeral it was.
