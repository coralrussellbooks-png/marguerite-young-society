Definition from research

"If the mountain will not come to Mahomet, Mahomet must go to the mountain" is Francis Bacon's proverb, a lesson in bending to circumstance when the world will not bend, dropped in as the Queen Maud Mountains seem to walk into Catherine's room.

Sir Walter Raleigh's floating head raises the old heresy that "man had existed before Adam," the pre-Adamite idea Raleigh entertained in his History of the World, that peoples might have been made before or apart from the Genesis account.

"God is dog spelled backward," another line from that head, is a small blasphemy built on a chance of English spelling, doubt dressed up as a word game.
