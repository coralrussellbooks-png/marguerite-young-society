In the research: Chapter 4, Chapter 7, Chapter 48, Chapter 62, Chapter 69

Definition from research

Chapter 4 · Driftwood House and Fire on the Beach

  • Symbolize Vera’s fragile hope and attempt to reach out to Miss MacIntosh or the lost past.

Chapter 7 · The Locked Door & Fire

  • Symbolizes inescapable fate, hidden crimes, and the destructive power of secrets.

Chapter 48 · Matches and Fire

  • Fire represents life, energy, and destruction. Mr. Spitzer’s fear of lighting matches contrasts with his brother’s flamboyant use of fire, symbolizing different attitudes toward life and death.

Chapter 62 · Dream of the River of Fire and Ice

  • Reflects the shifting states of reality and consciousness, the tension between light and darkness, life and death.

Chapter 69 · Fire Escape Room

  • Symbolizes escape but also confinement, a place between safety and danger.
