Part Three: The Monday Mirror

← Part Two: Common Questions · Radical Immanence Collection · Part Four: Short Meditations →

The Monday Mirror is a weekly practice of honest attention. Not self-improvement. Not reviewing your performance. Just turning and looking at what is actually here before the week carries you forward.

These thirty prompts are organized by theme. You can use one per week, one per day, or open the list wherever it opens. There is no order to complete. There is only the one you are in right now.

The question underneath every prompt is the same: what is actually here?

01 BODY

Notice where your shoulders are right now. Not where they should be. Where they actually are. If they are up near your ears, that is information. It is not a problem to fix. You have been carrying something. Your body already knows. You do not need to explain it. You can just let your shoulders drop a little, or not. Either way, something true is here.

What is here for you in this?

02 MORNING

You woke up today and the first feeling was not gratitude. Maybe it was dread, or a blank gray nothing, or the weight of what is still unresolved. That is not a failure of your morning. That is your morning. It is already happening. It counts. You are already in the day.

What is here for you in this?

03 GRIEF

The person you lost would have liked today's weather, or they would have hated it. Either way, you noticed. That noticing is not a wound to push through. It is a form of love that is still moving in you. It does not need to go anywhere. It is allowed to just be here, in the noticing.

What is here for you in this?

04 ORDINARY OBJECT

The mug on your desk is probably not clean. Or it has been sitting there long enough that the coffee left a ring. That ring is time. You were here yesterday, and the day before. You kept showing up, even on the days that did not deserve it. The ring is proof.

What is here for you in this?

05 INCOMPLETION

There is something you have not finished. Maybe many things. The list exists somewhere, maybe written down, maybe just living in your chest. You do not have to address it today. But you can look at it honestly. Incompletion is not the same as failure. Sometimes it is just the shape of a life being lived.

What is here for you in this?

06 RELATIONSHIP

Think of someone you love and have not called. You know who it is. The not-calling is not indifference. It might be exhaustion. It might be not knowing what to say. It might be that connection feels too large right now. That is allowed. The love is still there, even when the phone is not in your hand.

What is here for you in this?

07 SEASON

It is the season it is. Not the one you wish it were. The light is exactly this long today, and in your body something responds to that whether you think about it or not. You are an animal on a tilted planet. Something in you is adjusting. That is not poetic. It is just true.

What is here for you in this?

08 EVENING

At the end of the day you are counting what you did not do. That is a habit, not a verdict. The day happened. You moved through it. Some of it was good and some was barely manageable and some you would rather not look at. All of it was yours. All of it counts.

What is here for you in this?

09 MEMORY

Something from years ago has come up again this week. You recognize the shape of it. The feeling is older than the current situation. You are not broken for being back here. Some things circle. You can stand in the circle and just look at what is in the center.

What is here for you in this?

10 BODY

Your jaw is probably clenched. Not from anger, necessarily. Just from the effort of being a person today. Let it soften, if you can. If you cannot, that is fine too. Notice the effort itself. You have been working hard at something. It shows up in the body. The body is not wrong.

What is here for you in this?

11 GRIEF

You are grieving something no one else can see. A version of a relationship that did not become what you hoped. A door that closed quietly. No one sent flowers. There was no ceremony. But it was real, and the loss is real. You are allowed to grieve things that do not have funerals.

What is here for you in this?

12 MORNING

You did not sleep well. Or you slept too long and woke up feeling far from yourself. Either way, you are here now, in this body, on this particular day. You do not have to generate energy you do not have. You can just begin where you actually are.

What is here for you in this?

13 ORDINARY OBJECT

Look at your hands. Not to evaluate them. Just look. These are the hands that have done every hard thing you have done. They have reached out, held on, let go, made things, and sometimes done nothing at all when nothing was all you had. They are enough hands.

What is here for you in this?

14 INCOMPLETION

You started something and stopped. You meant to return to it. It has been longer than you planned. This is not a story about your character. This is a story about capacity, and seasons, and the way life interrupts itself. The thing you stopped is still there if you want it. And it is okay if you do not.

What is here for you in this?

15 RELATIONSHIP

There is someone you are not sure how to be around right now. Something shifted and it has not been named. You are carrying the weight of an unspoken thing. You do not have to speak it today. But you can notice the weight. It is real, and noticing it is not nothing.

What is here for you in this?

16 SEASON

Something is ending. Maybe slowly, maybe in a way you can only see in retrospect. Endings do not always announce themselves. Sometimes they are just a gradual quieting. You are allowed to feel the ending before you understand it. Understanding is not required for feeling.

What is here for you in this?

17 BODY

There is tiredness in you that sleep has not fixed. Not every tiredness is about sleep. Some of it is the weight of ongoing things: a situation with no resolution, a decision still unmade, a need that is not being met. You are not lazy. You are carrying something real.

What is here for you in this?

18 MEMORY

You remember a moment when you felt genuinely okay. Not happy, necessarily. Just okay, and present, and not braced against anything. That moment was real. You have been that okay before. You may be that okay again. You do not have to perform okayness now to remember it exists.

What is here for you in this?

19 GRIEF

Grief is not always loud. Sometimes it is just a Tuesday afternoon when everything is technically fine and you feel a weight you cannot name. The weight is not nothing. It is accumulated. It is real. You do not have to explain it to anyone, including yourself. You can just be under it for a moment.

What is here for you in this?

20 ORDINARY OBJECT

The photo on your phone that you keep coming back to. You know which one. There is something in it that you need. Maybe it is the person in it. Maybe it is the version of you that took it. Maybe it is just that particular afternoon. Whatever it is, it is still here, in that square of light.

What is here for you in this?

21 MORNING

You have things to do today and part of you does not want to do any of them. That part is not sabotage. It might be asking for something. Rest. Acknowledgment. A moment that belongs to no task. You might not be able to give it that today. But you can at least hear the asking.

What is here for you in this?

22 INCOMPLETION

You have been meaning to forgive yourself for something. It has been a while. Forgiveness is not a decision you make once. It is something that happens slowly, in layers, when you stop demanding that you already be on the other side of it. You are on this side. That is where the work is.

What is here for you in this?

23 RELATIONSHIP

There is someone who loves you in a way you find difficult to receive. Not because the love is wrong, but because receiving requires something open in you that is sometimes closed. You are not ungrateful. You are human. Let the love be there, even if you cannot fully take it in today.

What is here for you in this?

24 BODY

Your stomach. What is happening there right now. Tightness, or emptiness, or a low-grade hum of something unresolved. The gut knows before the mind does. It is not catastrophizing. It is tracking something real. You can check in with it, the way you would check in with a friend.

What is here for you in this?

25 SEASON

You are in a period of waiting. Not the romantic kind. The kind where you cannot move forward yet and you cannot go back and the middle is uncomfortable and slow. You are not behind. You are in a season that requires patience you did not plan to need. It is still a season. It will not last forever.

What is here for you in this?

26 EVENING

Before you go to sleep tonight, you will probably run through everything that went wrong, or almost wrong, or could go wrong tomorrow. That is the mind doing its job. You do not have to believe all of it. You can watch it the way you watch weather. Real, but not the whole story.

What is here for you in this?

27 MEMORY

There is a younger version of you who needed something they did not get. Not because no one cared, or maybe exactly because no one cared. Either way, the need was real and it is still in you somewhere. You can acknowledge that today without needing it to resolve. Some things we just carry, and that is allowed.

What is here for you in this?

28 GRIEF

You are not the same as you were before the thing that happened. You know that. Sometimes people want you to be, or you want yourself to be, and the gap between who you were and who you are now is the grief. You do not have to bridge it. You can just stand in it and look both ways.

What is here for you in this?

29 ORDINARY OBJECT

The plant in your window. The pile on your floor. The light coming in at this particular angle today. This is the texture of your actual life. Not the version you are building toward. This one. The mess and the light together. Here. This is where you live.

What is here for you in this?

30 INCOMPLETION

You are not finished becoming. That is not a promise that something better is coming. It is just the truth of being alive. You are mid-sentence. Most people are. The incompletion is not the problem. It is the condition. And within it, right now, you are enough.

What is here for you in this?
