Characters: Joachim Spitzer · Peron Spitzer

Definition from research

Mr. Spitzer buries his brother with the solemnity of a man who believes death is a formal occasion, and with the grave goods of a gambler: two poker chips, a red and a black, for the ferryman, two dice showing only sevens and elevens, a racing form, and the six of hearts under his folded hands. He even paid an old billiard hall artist to come and take one last shot.

The music is the comic crown. A one-armed violinist plays one squeak, and the official track bugler from Suffolk Downs and Pimlico, who has also been first trumpet of the Boston, Philadelphia, and Minneapolis symphonies, who sometimes played at all the great symphonies at once, plays the starter’s call at the grave. The funeral is perfectly decorous and perfectly ridiculous, a portrait of both brothers at once.
