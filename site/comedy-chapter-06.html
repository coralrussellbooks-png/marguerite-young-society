Definition from research

The surreal household comedy peaks in the invalid mother's imaginary society. She converts the furniture into company, greeting the chandelier as "Mr. Chandelier" and the drug bottle as "Mr. Res Tacamah," and she torments the long-suffering Mr. Spitzer with unanswerable questions, most famously whether "there is or is not an invisible elephant in the corridor leading to this bedroom," then reasoning that if there is no elephant, what is one doing in the house. Her rooms fill with drowned Egyptians, dead kings, and men who are birds, all treated as ordinary callers who must be offered refreshments. The joke is her impeccable social manner extended to a guest list of pure hallucination, hospitality without a single real guest.
