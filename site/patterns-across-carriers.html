The begats

Material: Genealogies of the tribes of Israel

What happens to it: Miss MacIntosh drills them (ch. 9), the bus-driver recites them as a lament (ch. 7), and in chapter 18 Miss MacIntosh herself asks "Where is Issachar... Where is Reuben?" and adds "Where is Mr. Spitzer?" The memory drill becomes a roll call of the lost.

Where: Issachar (9, 10, 18, 22); Reuben (7, 10, 18)

"Where is Issachar, my dear, my dear of dears?" (ch. 18)

The ferryman

Material: Charon, Styx, Hades

What happens to it: The underworld crossing passes from Catherine's visions to the bus-driver's joke about the fare, to Hannah's travels, to Mr. Spitzer's purse. The bus itself is a ferry of the dead.

Where: Charon (3, 5, 8, 30, 46, 51, 52, 53, 59, 82)

The last trumpet

Material: Gabriel's horn

What happens to it: A shared oath across every class: bus-driver, Doc's circle, Catherine, Mr. Spitzer, and the stranger in chapter 68.

Where: Gabriel (1, 5, 7, 30, 39, 51, 68, 82)

Invented time

Material: Constantine's calendar, the Hebrew calendar, Candlemas, Maundy Thursday

What happens to it: Time appears as something people built and can change. Miss MacIntosh blames Constantine for putting "us all off on the wrong day of the week," and the grievance seems to come from Bonebreaker's preaching.

Where: Constantine (22, 23, 63, 64, 65, 66); Hebrew calendar (20)

Borrowed lineage

Material: Pedigree claims

What happens to it: Esther (Washington's sister), Mrs. Hogden (Boone, William the Conqueror, Bell), Bonebreaker (Hapsburg thrones), and Catherine's Cleopatra neighbors all claim descent from or identity with grand names.

Where: Chapters 26, 65, 70, 78

Doubling and crossed gender

Material: Twins, pairs, swapped roles

What happens to it: Two Cleopatras, twin portraits, one canary with nine names, the bus-driver as Penelope, Hannah as Jason and as either Tristram or Yseult, Esther as Christ.

Where: Chapters 2, 5, 26, 42, 52, 55, 79

Parted lovers

Material: Romeo and Juliet, Paul and Virginia, Eloise and Abelard, Tristram and Yseult

What happens to it: The same list of lovers appears with Madge (6), Mr. Spitzer (52) and Miss MacIntosh (65), who reads it as a reminder of "the increase of American divorces."

Where: Chapters 6, 52, 65
