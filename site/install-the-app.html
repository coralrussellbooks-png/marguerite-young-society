Install the App

From The Marguerite Young Society, a free companion to the life and work of Marguerite Young (1908 to 1995).
By Coral Russell · Updated November 23, 2026

The Marguerite Young Society · All pages

The Marguerite Young Society can be installed on a phone, tablet, or computer and used like any other app. It gets its own icon, opens full screen without the browser's address bar, and keeps the pages it opens on the device, so they open again without a connection. It is free, there is nothing to sign up for, and it updates itself whenever the site changes.

On an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. Tap "Add." The Marguerite Young icon appears on the home screen.

On an Android phone or tablet

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (the three dots at the top right).
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen."
  4. Tap "Install." The Marguerite Young icon appears on the home screen.

On a computer

  1. Open this site in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with a downward arrow). If it is not there, open the browser's menu and look for "Install" (in Chrome it is under "Cast, save, and share").
  3. Click "Install." The site opens in its own window and can be pinned to the taskbar or dock.

On a Mac, Safari can do the same: open the site, then choose File, then "Add to Dock."

Reading without a connection

Every page opened in the app is kept on the device, so it opens again even with no signal. To keep a whole book ready ahead of time, open its contents page and tap "Save this book offline." That button is on these books:

  • To All My Darlings
  • A Guide to Reading Miss MacIntosh, My Darling
  • The Wheel and the Spokes
  • The Deep Architecture of Miss MacIntosh, My Darling
  • The Philosophy of Marguerite Young’s Miss MacIntosh, My Darling
  • History as Something Done in Miss MacIntosh, My Darling
  • Intertextuality in Miss MacIntosh, My Darling
  • Exploring Reality and Illusion in Miss MacIntosh, My Darling

Save the whole site

To keep everything on the device, every page, every picture, and the full-text search, use the button below. It works in the app or in the browser. The whole site is large, so a Wi-Fi connection is best, and it can take several minutes. If the connection drops partway, tap the button again to finish.

The EPUB and PDF editions of the books are not part of this save. Download them from each book's contents page to read in any eBook app.

Staying up to date

Whenever there is a connection, the app shows the newest version of each page, and saved pages are refreshed as they are opened. To remove the app, delete it the way any other app is deleted: press and hold the icon on a phone, or choose "Uninstall" from the app's menu on a computer.
