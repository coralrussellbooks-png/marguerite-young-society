Pillar: Pillar 7 The Margins Reveal the Truth · Across every book

Who did you file as background today, and what did that cost them in your mind?

The hunger is to be at the center, to matter, and underneath that, to look down on whoever is not. Power builds a center and a margin and trades you a place in the center for your contempt toward the edge. This pillar grants full being to the marginal, so the machine of center and enemy loses its grip.

Notice

Name one person you passed today who felt like background, a cashier, someone on the street, a coworker whose name you do not know. Notice how quickly you filed them as minor.

Practice

Write a short paragraph imagining that person's actual day, with the same seriousness you would give a main character in a story. Give them a history, a worry, a person who loves them.

Reflection

Where do you place yourself, center or edge, in most of your daily thinking?

Whose contempt does that placement quietly require of you?
