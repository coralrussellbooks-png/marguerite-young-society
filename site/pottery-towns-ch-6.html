From the guide

Pottery towns of the Midwest. East Liverpool, Ohio was one of the great ceramic centers of America, the “Pottery Capital of the World” at its peak in the early twentieth century, producing everything from washbowls to fine china. The clay soils of eastern Ohio and western Pennsylvania made this industry possible. Madge working in a pottery town is a small, precise detail that places her in a specific working-class Midwestern world, a world of making things with your hands, of practical necessity, far from the opium dreams of the Cartwheel household.
