Characters: Esther Longtree · Mrs. Hogden

Definition from research

The dark comedy braids its several grotesques together. The landlord and the hangman keep up their colorful, peculiar talk, the hangman's stories growing stranger, the landlord's grumbling growing sourer. Mrs. Hogden's frivolous self-absorption and Esther Longtree's tragicomic history sit alongside them, so that the chapter becomes a small gallery of Young's comic-grotesque types, each one funny and stricken at once. The humor is cumulative, a chorus of oddities whose combined effect is the book's peculiar mixture of the ridiculous and the mournful.
