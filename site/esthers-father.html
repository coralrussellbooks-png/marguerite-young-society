In the research: Chapter 74, Chapter 75, Chapter 78, Chapter 79

Definition from research

Chapter 74

Listed as: Esther’s Father (the Policeman)

  • Symbolizes familial and societal surveillance and judgment.
  • His passive role highlights Esther’s isolation and the inescapability of the small-town environment.

Chapter 75

  • A policeman, symbolizing authority and possibly complicity; Esther loves him from a distance but fears implications about paternity.

Chapter 78

Listed as: Esther’s father

  • He is a passive policeman, seen as a lonely, timid man whose life is confined and unfulfilled. He patrols the graveyard to warn off lovers to avoid arrests, embodying a man trapped by circumstance and routine. Her mother is a small, childlike woman with little understanding of life or Esther’s struggles. Their marriage lacks love, and Esther inherits nothing from her mother but much from her father’s robust, natural lineage.

Chapter 79

Listed as: Esther’s Father (The Policeman)

  • A large, distant man who never consummated the marriage and remained emotionally aloof.
  • He is passive, absorbed in his own interests, such as reading old baseball scores.
  • His relationship with Esther’s mother is marked by distance and lack of physical union.
