One page per chapter, with a summary and links to every version of that chapter across your notes.

Each chapter is marked with its length so you can plan your reading. Short chapters are under about 0.6% of the novel, medium chapters run up to about 1.2%, long chapters up to about 2%, and very long chapters more. The longest, Chapter 6, is about 4.4% of the book, and the shortest, Chapter 11, is under a thousand words. Lengths are measured against the whole novel, so they hold for any edition.
