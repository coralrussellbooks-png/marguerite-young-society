From the guide

On opium.

By the late 1800s, women made up sixty percent of opium addicts in America. It was widely prescribed for everything from menstrual pain to “nervous complaints.” Catherine is not an anomaly, she is a portrait of what happened to women for whom the world offered no other exit. Young knew this history and writes Catherine without judgment, only with attention.
