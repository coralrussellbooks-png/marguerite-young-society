In the research: Chapter 82

Definition from research

Chapter 82

  • A poignant figure who cannot hear but communicates and experiences the world through other senses. He has a profound inner life and love beyond words. Young said that “the deaf are normal in every way” and that those who lack a sense “develop a hypersensitivity in every other way” (Marguerite Young, interview with Studs Terkel, WFMT radio, 1965). The woman he is with when Vera first meets him is Lucie Bell.
