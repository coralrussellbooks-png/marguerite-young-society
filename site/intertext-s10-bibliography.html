Definition from research

The novel is cited throughout by chapter, not by page. The entries below are grouped under five headings:

  • the novel;

  • theory;

  • source texts named, quoted, or evoked in the novel;

  • songs and music;

  • reader connections that Young does not cite.

Where this volume quotes a source directly, the edition or translation listed is the one to consult.
