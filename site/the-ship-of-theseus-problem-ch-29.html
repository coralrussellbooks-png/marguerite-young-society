The Ship of Theseus problem (Ch 29)

On Chapter 29 of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965). Part of A Guide to Reading Miss MacIntosh, My Darling.
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

From the reading guide, Chapter 29 · Research: Chapter 29

From the guide

The Ship of Theseus problem, which underlies the sock question, is one of philosophy’s oldest paradoxes, recorded in Plutarch: if a ship has all its planks replaced one by one, is it still the same ship? Thomas Hobbes extended it: if you take all the original planks and build a second ship from them, which one is the Ship of Theseus? Young applies this to identity, to grief, to the question of whether Joachim Spitzer is still Joachim Spitzer after so many years of being patched with pieces of his dead brother’s life.
