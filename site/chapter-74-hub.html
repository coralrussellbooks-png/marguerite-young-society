Chapter 74: The Chicago Detective

← Chapter 73 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 75 →

Content note: The Esther Longtree chapters (72 to 81) deal with stillbirth and pregnancy loss, rape, infanticide, racist violence including lynching, and cruelty to children. This chapter is one of the hardest in the novel: Esther describes being raped by the Chicago detective, and the chapter deals with racism.

Story arc: Arc 8 Esther Longtree

Length: Medium, about 1.1% of the novel (7,715 words)

Character hubs: Joachim Spitzer · Esther Longtree · The Chicago Detective · Esther’s Father · The Old Woman Abortionist

Summary

Esther dreads a trial before a jury. The Chicago detective has come three times in three disguises, collecting clues for murders no one else notices. He is also the man who raped her. He cares only about the deaths of white children. Esther's speech begins to repeat words in threes, and she hints at an old, hidden death from her youth. Her own childhood reproaches her.
