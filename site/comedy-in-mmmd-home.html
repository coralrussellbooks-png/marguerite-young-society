The other chapters

Comedy runs through almost the whole novel, often in the same sentence as grief. In a few chapters it steps back almost entirely: 13, 17, 19, 20, 28, 37, 41, 45, 47, 49, 74. These include the night and morning when Vera discovers what Miss MacIntosh has hidden (Chapters 17, 19, and 20), Mr. Spitzer’s quietest meditations, and Esther’s account of the detective in Chapter 74, though even there she laughs at having fooled him.
