Definition from research

Vera remembers a stretch of childhood when she pretended to be a dog and dug for bones in the garden, and Miss MacIntosh restored her to her senses by “solving most problems by ignoring them.” Her creed comes in comic absolutes: even if she were dead, she would not waste her time dreaming. “No, siree, sir.” She is middle brow, Middle Western, and middle aged, a woman with one face and no spare.

The chapter closes on Catherine’s suspicion about Mr. Spitzer. Sometimes, she thinks, he imagines himself to be his dead brother and, with a remarkable knowledge of horse flesh for a man who knows nothing of it, bets on the dark horse that will come in first, a phantom like himself, while chatting with dead jockeys from beyond the Alps. The gambler’s instinct turns up in the timid twin as a ghost’s inheritance.
