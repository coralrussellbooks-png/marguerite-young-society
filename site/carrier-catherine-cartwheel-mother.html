Catherine Cartwheel (mother) as Carrier

Intertextual Index Home

Character: Catherine Cartwheel

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Imaginary guests: Pope, Byron, Shelley, Milton, Ben Jonson, Plato, Pythagoras, Rhadamanthus, Spinoza, Shakespeare, Jenny Lind, the Wandering Jew, Dante, Diderot; the alphabetical P's and Q's; one canary with many names; the beds of history; calls to the dead.

Pattern

She hosts history. Her references are guests to be seated, rooms to be assigned, and calls that go unanswered (the Bishop of Hippo "had just stepped out," Hadrian is in his garden, Canute is on a voyage). The canon enters through its wounds and humiliations. Her single canary answers to Juniper, Tulip, Cellini, Solomon, Methuselah, Marco Polo, Peary, Shackleton and Dante, which makes her the book's clearest carrier of the plural self.

Key references (chapters)

Visitors (2, 11, 14, 24, 40); P's and Q's (9, 11); canary names (2); unanswered calls (24); Sunt lacrimae rerum (41); easeful death (40); Volpone's imaginary death (40).

Line from the novel

"Spinoza and Shakespeare and Mrs. Sydington were already in the green." (ch. 24)
