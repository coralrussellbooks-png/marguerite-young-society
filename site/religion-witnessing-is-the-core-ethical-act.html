Characters: Joachim Spitzer · Vera Cartwheel

Definition from research

If grief cannot be fixed, what remains is to stay present with it, and this is the office both Vera and Mr. Spitzer perform. Vera is the novel's witness, the recorder who holds every contradiction, every dream and denial, without rushing them to a conclusion. Her long quest for a "truth that does not fail" ends not in an answer but in an acceptance of ambivalence, which radical immanence would call the mark of an honest witness rather than a defeat. Mr. Spitzer serves the dead directly. He is executor, companion to the dying, seeker of lost heirs and lost souls, a man whose whole vocation is to attend to what cannot be repaired. His compassion is not effective in any worldly sense. It is presence, offered anyway, which the philosophy names as the hardest and most central ethical act.
