Definition from research

Time in the novel does not recede. It accumulates, layer on layer, pressing on the present the way radical immanence describes history as a geological fact rather than a closed event. Vera is made to memorize the genealogies, the long biblical descent of names, so that the dead of scripture become sediment underfoot. Miss MacIntosh will not forgive Emperor Constantine for "wrecking the old creation," for confusing the calendar and moving the day of rest, an ancient administrative act she treats as a wound still open in the present week. The dead are never behind the living in this book. They are underneath them, and no one can build fairly on ground pretended to be neutral.
