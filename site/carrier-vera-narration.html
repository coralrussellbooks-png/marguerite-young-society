Intertextual Index Home

Character: Vera Cartwheel

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Don Quixote, Tantalus, ships in the night, deep calling to deep, dragons' teeth, Lazarus, Falada, a dream within a dream (with Othello's men whose heads grow beneath their shoulders), the primers and pamphlets at the doctor's house.

Pattern

Allusions of perception and reaching. Vera's own references are few (about 65 read rows) because most of what she reports belongs to others. The ones she owns describe the act of seeing and not grasping, and they frame everyone else's clusters.

Key references (chapters)

Chapter 1; Lazarus (4); Falada (16); primers and books (71).

Line from the novel

"much like the myth of Tantalus" (ch. 1)
