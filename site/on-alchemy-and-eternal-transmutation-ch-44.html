On alchemy and eternal transmutation.

The alchemical tradition sought to transmute base metals into gold, not because gold was the point, but because the transmutation was understood as a spiritual process, a refinement of matter toward its highest potential. Paracelsus, one of alchemy's great practitioners, said the goal was not material wealth but the transformation of the self through the understanding of transformation in matter. Young's use of the word transmutation for death places Cousin Hannah's dying within this tradition: not annihilation but refinement. Not ending but becoming. The lead becomes gold. The woman becomes the myth. The myth becomes the novel. The novel becomes what you are reading right now.
