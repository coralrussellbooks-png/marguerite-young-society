On the dreamer question and Descartes.

René Descartes asked, in his Meditations, how we know we are not dreaming right now: how we know that our waking experience is not simply a more coherent version of what happens in sleep. He could not find a reliable answer. Young knew her Descartes. The question "but who was the dreamer?" is Cartesian in structure: it does not ask what is being dreamed but who is doing the dreaming, which is the deeper and more unsettling question. If you cannot identify the dreamer, you cannot establish a stable ground for anything else.
