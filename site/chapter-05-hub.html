Chapter 5: A Crazy Bus-Driver Losing His Way That He Knew Too Well

← Chapter 4 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 6 →

Story arc: Arc 1 The Bus Ride

Length: Medium, about 1.2% of the novel (7,970 words)

Character hubs: Vera Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh · Moses Hunnecker · Justice O’Leary · Madge Capehorn · Homer Capehorn · Catherine Cartwheel

Summary

Night falls on the bus. The roads are flooded and there are no stars or signposts. The bus waits at a Pennsylvania Railroad crossing while Madge moans in her sleep, accusing every man but her husband. Moses sings, drinks, and holds forth. He refuses ever to cut his hair. He rails against the Democrats and the New Deal, though he never has time to vote. He lives with his old mother, has never married, and talks constantly of pregnant women and of old Doc O'Leary, who once delivered babies and has now lost his mind. Birth and death fill his talk. The chapter leaves open whether Moses is driving the bus or only dreaming that he drives it.
