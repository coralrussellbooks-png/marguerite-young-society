From the guide

On the aubade.

An aubade is a morning love song, specifically one sung at the parting of lovers at dawn: the form that acknowledges that the night of love is ending and the day, with all its demands and separations, is beginning. Mr. Spitzer's music, in the chapter, is described as a kind of aubade or elegy that cannot quite awaken the dead. The aubade is the form for the ending that was always coming, the form for loving in the knowledge that morning will arrive and you will have to let go. Cousin Hannah's life was an aubade: the dawn always coming, the separation always imminent, the holding-on and the letting-go simultaneous.
