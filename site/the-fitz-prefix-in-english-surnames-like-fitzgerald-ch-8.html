The Fitz prefix in English surnames like Fitzgerald (Ch 8)

From the reading guide, Chapter 8 · Research: Chapter 8

From the guide

The “Fitz” prefix in English surnames like Fitzgerald, Fitzroy, and Fitzwilliam does trace back to the Norman-French word fils (son of), and several famous “Fitz” surnames were associated with royal illegitimate lines , most notably the Fitzroys, descendants of King Charles II and his mistresses. Homer’s claim that Beatrice has “blue blood” through her name is a piece of village mythology, the kind of genealogical rumor that attaches itself to people on the margins and grants them a dignity their circumstances deny them. Young takes it seriously as a form of the human need to believe that the overlooked are secretly remarkable. She thinks they are. She just doesn’t think it should need to be a secret.
