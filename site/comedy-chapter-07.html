Definition from research

Old Doc’s car is the chapter’s comic emblem. Moses sees the wheelless frame of the Model T by the road and runs through everything the doctor no longer needs: no steering wheel, no tires, no skid chain, no license plates, no motor. The list is funny because it keeps going, and because its conclusion is that a car with nothing left is the perfect vehicle for visiting patients who are ghosts.

Inside the bus, Madge tells the darkest stories of their hometown, the Hathaway brothers and the locked door and the fire, and Homer answers with his stomach. He is hungry, his stomach is roaring, and he wants a hamburger at Josh’s All Night Spot. Madge’s refusal to set foot in that drinking joint, with its poolroom sharks and wild women, turns a murder story into a domestic squabble. Homer’s appetite is the book’s steady comic counterweight to Madge’s gloom.
