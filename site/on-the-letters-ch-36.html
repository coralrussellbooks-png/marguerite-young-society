On the letters.

Mr. Spitzer writes letters that never arrive. Catherine writes letters that are illegible. Young is making something very precise here about two different failures of communication. Mr. Spitzer's failure is one of delivery: the intention is there, the effort is made, but nothing reaches its destination. Catherine's failure is one of legibility: the effort is made, but what emerges cannot be read. These are not the same failure. One is a postal problem. The other is a problem of language itself, of whether what is inside can ever be made available to the outside at all.
