Definition from research

The needle's eye returns, the camel that cannot pass, bent again toward birth.

"so she was Jesus Christ! So she had come into the world in a most peculiar way, only after long trial, long suffering" likens Esther's strange, suffering birth to Christ's, half claim and half irony.
