The Northwest Passage (Ch 32)

From the reading guide, Chapter 32 · Research: Chapter 32

From the guide

The Northwest Passage appears near the chapter’s end as Young’s image for the impossible route every soul must seek: that narrow crevice in the ice suddenly opening into “great abysses filled with cities of frozen pinnacles and towers.” The Northwest Passage was the legendary sea route through the Arctic that explorers sought for centuries and that cost many lives. Young makes it the name for what every person ultimately has to navigate: the passage between what is known and what cannot be known, between partial knowledge and the full darkness beyond.
