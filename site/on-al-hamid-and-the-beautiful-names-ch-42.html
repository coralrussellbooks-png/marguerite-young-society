On al Hamid and the Beautiful Names.

In Islamic tradition, Allah has ninety-nine Beautiful Names, each describing a divine attribute. Al Hamid (the Praiseworthy, the Laudable, the object of all praise) is among them. That Young folds this name into Cousin Hannah's accumulated identities, in a chapter that also hints at her possible conversion and subsequent abandonment of faith, is an act of precise and serious literary compression. Hannah becomes Hamid becomes Al Hamid: the New England suffragette takes on the name of divine praise in a tradition she may have briefly inhabited and then departed. The name remains. It is one of the most beautiful things a person can be called.
