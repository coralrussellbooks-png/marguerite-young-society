Inviting the Muses: Stories, Essays, Reviews

← Harp Song for a Radical · The Marguerite Young Society · The Collected Poems of Marguerite Young →

Published: Dalkey Archive Press, 1994. 246 pages.

A gathering of Young's shorter work: three short stories, one of them previously unpublished; fifteen essays, including "On Teaching"; twenty-three book reviews; and character sketches of Johnny Gruelle, the creator of Raggedy Ann, and the poet Marianne Moore.

The reviews show what she valued in other writers. She praises authors who work through symbolism and mysticism, such as Truman Capote and Doris Lessing, and argues against Jean-Paul Sartre's view that life is meaningless.
