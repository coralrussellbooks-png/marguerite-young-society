Characters: Vera Cartwheel

Allusions: Genesis · The Bible

Definition from research

"that holy angel with whom Jacob struggled at Peniel" is Genesis 32, the night Jacob wrestles the divine stranger and comes away renamed Israel, marked and limping.

Vera's cry for her mother joins the book to the biblical cry of the helpless, children calling out in famine (Lamentations 2:12) and Christ entrusting his mother from the cross (John 19), the turn toward the first comfort at the last extremity.
