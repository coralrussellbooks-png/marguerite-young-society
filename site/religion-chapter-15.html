Allusions: Ecclesiastes · Revelation and the Apocalypse

Definition from research

"the plain wisdom of that old Ecclesiastes," set beside Exodus and the Farmer's Almanac, puts the preacher of vanity next to the book of deliverance and a book of weather, sacred and homely wisdom in one breath. Ecclesiastes' refrain that all is vanity sits behind it.

"seas of glass which man shall behold at the end of time," when "the sky rolls back like a scroll," gathers Revelation's sea of glass and its heavens rolled away, the imagery of the last day.

"this earth was but the testing ground," where "all should fail the test," states the book's recurring creed, life as a trial no one passes, close to the Christian sense of universal fallenness.
