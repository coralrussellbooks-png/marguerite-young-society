The heavy (Ch 18)

From the reading guide, Chapter 18 · Research: Chapter 18

From the guide

“The heavy, claw-shaped mark of her hand upon my forehead” is the chapter’s last concrete physical image, and it echoes the silver thimble engraved Juliet, 1899 that Miss MacIntosh used to tap against Vera’s forehead to restore her to her senses. The thimble was the instrument of practical correction. The claw-shaped mark is something different: a branding, a claim, a last act of possession before the night ends. Remember me. Be charitable. Both.
