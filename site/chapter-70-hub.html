Chapter 70: Mrs. Hogden

← Chapter 69 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 71 →

Story arc: Arc 7 The Tavern

Length: Long, about 1.9% of the novel (12,776 words)

Character hubs: Vera Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh · Catherine Cartwheel · Joachim Spitzer · Cousin Hannah · Mrs. Hogden · Mr. Res Tacamah · James the Chauffeur

Summary

In the quiet, Vera tells of Mrs. Hogden, whose name keeps shifting and whom Vera admits she never actually saw. Mrs. Hogden sold her Dutch windmill home and arrived at Catherine's house with birdcages, temple bells, and a letter of employment as arithmetic teacher that Catherine denied writing. Lisa Lunde, Catherine's new companion, suspected her at once. Mrs. Hogden lived for months in the abandoned playroom, playing chess against herself. She finally left her wig on a crab at the water's edge and a blackboard message reading "All a's are b's."
