On Groundhog Day, Candlemas, and the circular year.

Both Groundhog Day and Candlemas fall on February 2nd: the one a secular American folk tradition, the other the Christian feast of the Presentation of Jesus and the purification of Mary, itself descended from the Roman festival of Lupercalia. Young's reach for this date, where the American and the sacred and the ancient Roman all coincide, is characteristic: she is always finding the places where different calendars overlay each other, where the same day has been holy or significant in systems that have nothing else in common. Catherine experiences time this way: as simultaneous layers of meaning rather than a single sequential track. Every date is Candlemas and Groundhog Day and Lupercalia at once.
