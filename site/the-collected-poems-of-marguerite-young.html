The Collected Poems of Marguerite Young

← Inviting the Muses · The Marguerite Young Society · Psychology in Young's Works →

Published: Sublunary Editions and Chatwin Books, 2022, edited by Phil Bevis and Joshua Rothes.

Young's first two books were poetry, published while she was still teaching high school English in Indianapolis. This edition gathers every poem of hers that survives: Prismatic Ground (1937) in full, Moderate Fable (1944), early uncollected verse, poems written between the two books, a section of late poems from 1945, and drafts and sketches drawn from the Marguerite Young Papers at Yale.

The poems favor the natural world, especially animals and birds, and keep blurring the line between nature and abstraction. Angels recur, and so do questions of reality and memory that lead straight into the novel. One line from "Elegy" could stand for all her work: "Reality is wild and on the wing."
