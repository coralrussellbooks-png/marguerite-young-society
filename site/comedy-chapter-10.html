Definition from research

Unable to picture her own wedding, Vera tries to marry off the adults instead, and the candidates keep failing on technical grounds: Catherine rejected Mr. Spitzer long ago in favor of his brother, who did not want her. When the wedding finally arrives in her dream, the casting is the joke. Mr. Spitzer is the bride in a white veil, and Miss MacIntosh is the bridegroom, wearing a waxed mustache like his, a high silk hat, and her silver dollar watch.

The guest list completes it: Vera, who is not herself, Solomon the turtle, the red geranium, and an old, empty shoe. There is no preacher in sight. Young lets the scene be funny for exactly as long as it takes Vera to wake troubled, because the dream has told her something true about the two people who raised her: each is a little of the other, and neither is anyone’s spouse.
