On "Respice finem."

This Latin phrase (consider the end, or look to the end) appears in Mr. Spitzer's reflections and carries the weight of the chapter. In medieval and Renaissance thought it was a memento mori: remember that you will die, and let that knowledge shape how you live. Mr. Spitzer has been looking toward the end for many chapters now. The frog's burial is another occasion to look, and not look away.
