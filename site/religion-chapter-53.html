Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Philosophy: Pythagoras and the Music of the Spheres

Allusions: Astronomy

Definition from research

A "baffled look... as if suddenly an angel had passed" gives Mr. Spitzer the air of one brushed by the unseen and misunderstood by those around him.

He could sooner describe "Mt. Ararat, where Noah's Ark was supposed to have rested," than make himself understood.

"the roof of the Sistine Chapel, painted with angelic figures" sets his imagination among the great Christian art.

"the tombs of the Medicis," hung with "golden pawn-shop balls," blends the sacred vault with the pawnbroker's sign, itself once tied to Saint Nicholas.

His arithmetic of the heavens, "seven for the singing planets, one for the silent star, the black-out," sets seven, the number of completeness and the old music of the spheres, against a single dark, fallen star, harmony shadowed by loss.

"the pebble embedded in Goliath's forehead" recalls David and Goliath in 1 Samuel 17, the giant felled by a single stone, here likened to the moon in a cloud.
