A note on how this is arranged. It is based on The Collected Poems of Marguerite Young which can be purchased at Chatwin Books and I encourage you to do so.

Seven movements gather the one hundred and twenty-eight poems by the idea each one presses hardest on, not by the collection it came from. Within every movement the poems run across the same five periods in The Collected Poems book: Early Poems (1927 to 1934), Prismatic Ground (1937), Middle Years (1938 to 1943), Moderate Fable (1944), and Late Poems (1945).

Two of the eleven ideas, the Witness and Unfinished Is Honest, are not sorted here at all. They are the book’s method, the stance the readings keep and the openness the book admits about itself, and they run through every movement rather than owning one. A short list of the poems that sit on a seam between two movements closes the movements.
