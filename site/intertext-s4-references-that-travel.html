Definition from research

Some references pass from one carrier to another and change as they go. The genealogies of Israel move from Miss MacIntosh's drill to the bus-driver's lament to her own cry of loss. Charon's ferry and Gabriel's horn pass through every class. Constantine's calendar moves from Mr. Bonebreaker's sermon to Miss MacIntosh's grudge. Borrowed lineage, doubled roles, and the same list of parted lovers turn up in different hands. Section 5 follows these patterns as strategies, and Section 7 reads them through the principles of radical immanence.
