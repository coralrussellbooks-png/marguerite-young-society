Character: Justice O’Leary

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Barnum, Hagenbeck, the Bible as his wife quotes it, Jesus Christ and Jerusalem as a cry, the Calderon line.

Pattern

Show-business marvels and a broken cry. The weightiest philosophical line in the novel is his, spoken after he mistakes a real birth for a dream.

Key references (chapters)

Chapters 69, 77.

Line from the novel

"Life is a dream, and I have been wrong" (ch. 77)
