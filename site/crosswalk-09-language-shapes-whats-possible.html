Pillar 9 Language Shapes What's Possible

Every book's treatment of this pillar, side by side.

Language is not a neutral vehicle for truth. It shapes what can be seen, who can exist, what counts as real. Honest language names its own location. Language that pretends to speak from nowhere speaks from somewhere in particular.
