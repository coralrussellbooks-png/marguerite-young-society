Definition from research

History as Something Done: Marguerite Young's Miss MacIntosh, My Darling and the Philosophy of Radical Immanence

In this novel a historical reference is almost never a fact. It is an act. A governess uses it as armor, an opium dreamer invites it to tea, a lawyer grieves through it, a suffragette dresses in it, a stranger rewrites it, a sailor wears it on his skin. The philosophy set out in the reference document, radical immanence, turns out to describe these acts closely. The list, by stripping away the acts, erased the philosophy.
