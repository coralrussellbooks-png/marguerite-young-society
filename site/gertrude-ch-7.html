Gertrude (Ch 7)

From the reading guide, Chapter 7 · Research: Chapter 7

From the guide

Gertrude, the name Young gives to the woman accused of murdering her two husbands is also the name of Hamlet’s mother in Shakespeare, a woman caught between two men (her first husband and the brother who murdered him), whose moral position remains one of literature’s great ambiguities. Young is stacking the reference deliberately: her Gertrude is another woman whose guilt or innocence the novel refuses to settle, another woman whose life has been shaped entirely by men’s decisions, another figure standing at the intersection of marriage and murder.
