Definition from research

"God's wounds," the five wounds of the crucified Christ, lie behind the old oath and behind the marks of the stigmata.

"Defender of the faith" carries the double sense of God as protector and the believer charged to guard Christian doctrine.

Angels "blowing ivory horns in clouds" recall Revelation's trumpeting angels who herald judgment.

The "Tower of Babel" returns, the scattering of tongues from Genesis 11.

"Hebrew parables" points to the mashal, the wisdom-tale of Scripture.

"angels in all the angles" plays on Gregory the Great's pun, not Angles but angels.

The carriage wheels become "the sleeping seraphim," the highest angels of Isaiah's vision folded, dormant, into the spokes of a dream, the divine hidden in the ordinary.
