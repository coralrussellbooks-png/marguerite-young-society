Allusions: Genesis

Definition from research

Golden mountains sing "hallelujah," the word of praise.

He mourns "angelic faces in the vacuous clouds," angels glimpsed in an empty sky.

The whelks and wentletraps become "staircases to heaven," Jacob's ladder in a shell.

"Had he not given birth to death," "for a man has no womb," and "What if she is man?" turn on the Fall, death entering through sin, and unsettle the Genesis order of man and woman, the mother of all living, the sexes made distinct.

"if the dreamer was dead, how should he know," for "did not the dream live," and was not "this image mirrored on the deep," asks whether the soul outlasts the body and reaches back to Genesis 1, the Spirit moving on the deep.
