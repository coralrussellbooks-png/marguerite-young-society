Madge and Homer as Carrier

Part of Intertextual Index, a study of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965).
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

Intertextual Index Home

Character: Madge Capehorn · Homer Capehorn

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Romeo and Juliet, Helen of Troy, "Venus Adonis," Lincoln's sweetheart, Jenny Lind, Jack Robinson.

Pattern

Small-town mythmaking. The town reuses grand names for its most popular girl ("We elected her to be immortal"), and the heritage arrives misremembered, as in Madge's "Venus Adonis," a conflation of the armless Venus and Adonis.

Key references (chapters)

Chapter 6.

Line from the novel

"We elected her to be immortal." (ch. 6)
