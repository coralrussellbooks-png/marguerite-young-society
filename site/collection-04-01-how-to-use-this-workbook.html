How to Use This Workbook

← The Crossing Point: An Extension of Here Is Enough · Radical Immanence Collection · Part One: The Eleventh Idea →

This workbook is a companion to Here Is Enough. It does not replace it. It extends it along a new axis.

The Here Is Enough workbook pointed inward and downward, into the present moment, into the body, into what is already here. This workbook points in two new directions at once: down into the sediment of what made you, and forward into the ground you are forming for those who come after.

These are not abstract ideas. They are felt realities. You carry language deposited by people long dead. You carry grief that was not originally yours. You carry the particular shape of your particular history, in your body, in your assumptions, in the categories you use to make sense of the world. And everything you are doing right now is being deposited into the ground the not-yet-born will stand on.

The Crossing Point is where those two lines meet. It is where you are.

Work through this workbook in any order. Return to sections when they are speaking to you. Use the journal lines or do not. The practices are invitations, not requirements. The only requirement is honesty, which you are already practicing if you are here.
