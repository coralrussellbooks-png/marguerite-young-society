Chapter 60: The Wild Sonata

← Chapter 59 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 61 →

Story arc: Arc 5 Mr. Spitzer's Grief

Character hubs: Joachim Spitzer · Peron Spitzer

Summary

Mr. Spitzer wonders whether he is a musician at all or has really been a gambler, since he reads only the sporting pages. At dusk he practices moose calls in the city, and a moose seems to answer through the fog. Then wild ducks follow him for days, and he decides their cry can be his elegy.
