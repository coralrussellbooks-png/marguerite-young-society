Miss MacIntosh, My Darling

The Marguerite Young Society · Angel in the Forest →

Published: Scribner, 1965. Back in print from Dalkey Archive Press.

Young expected the novel to take about two years. It took eighteen. The manuscript grew to nearly 1,200 pages of long, looping sentences and dream logic, following a narrator who rides a bus across the Midwest in search of a lost nursemaid.
