Characters: Esther Longtree · Miss MacIntosh

Definition from research

Where Young does find the holy, it is never in the ornate. Miss MacIntosh loathes Rome's gold and pearl and keeps to a God who works with His hands. The angels of the book are made of barnacles, folded into carriage wheels, glimpsed as gulls over the water. "God loves the wind, the empty places." The sacred turns up at the margins, in the headhunter carving his saints, in Esther's grief, in the garden built for the blind, more often than in any church. This is an immanent religion, the holy located here, in the plain and the overlooked, the "poetic and cosmic" faith Young named as her own.
