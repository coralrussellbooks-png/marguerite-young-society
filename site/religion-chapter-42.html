Characters: Cousin Hannah

Definition from research

Death "came not with loud armies but came like a thief in the night" gathers the gospel warnings that the end comes unlooked-for (1 Thessalonians 5, Matthew 24), quiet and sudden.

"heaven opened like a door, she being that ivory door" makes Cousin Hannah the gate of heaven itself, a Peter holding the keys of the kingdom.

She trails "clouds of glory" and rises "into Christian paradise beyond these fields of asphodel," passing into the afterlife.

"should not all be as brides going to the bridegroom" makes every woman in death a bride of Christ, drawing on the marriage of the Lamb and the parable of the ten virgins.
