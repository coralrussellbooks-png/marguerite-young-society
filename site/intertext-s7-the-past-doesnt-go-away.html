Definition from research

"The past is not behind us. It is beneath us, pressed into the present like layers of rock."

In chapter 10 the tribes of Israel and the prairie states lie in a single layer: "Where is Manasses? Where is Kansas? Where is Iowa?" Troy, Babylon, and the Boston of the Lowells settle into the same fog.

The principle also warns that calling an arrangement inevitable hides the people who built it. Miss MacIntosh refuses to let that happen. She names the builder of the week she lives in, the Emperor Constantine, who "got us all off on the wrong day of the week" (chapter 22). The grievance seems to come from Mr. Bonebreaker, who preached a new creation where "there will be no time as in our present sense, no memory" (chapter 65). The essay "Invented Time" follows the novel's many calendars. Miss MacIntosh's comic grudge rests on a real perception: the calendar was made, and so it could have been made otherwise.
