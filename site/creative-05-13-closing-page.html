Eleven pillars, eleven hungers, eleven small practices. None of this makes the hungers go away. They are real, and they will come back, probably this week. What changes is that you now have somewhere else to put them besides the hands of whoever offers to resolve them for you.

A last exercise

Look back over what you wrote. Which pillar was hardest to sit with. Which one you kept wanting to skip. That is usually the one worth returning to first.
