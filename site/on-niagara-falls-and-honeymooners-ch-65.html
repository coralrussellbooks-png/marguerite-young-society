On Niagara Falls and honeymooners.

The falls have been a honeymoon destination since the early nineteenth century, when the Erie Canal and the railroad made them accessible. By Miss MacIntosh's time, the association was so fixed that the song "Shuffle Off to Buffalo", from the 1933 musical film 42nd Street, was already a popular shorthand for the honeymoon journey. Miss MacIntosh stands in the most famous beginning-place in America, alone, with an umbrella. Young uses this as only Young would: not for melodrama, but for the quiet, devastating precision of the contrast.
