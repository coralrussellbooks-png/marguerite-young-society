Characters: Esther Longtree

Definition from research

Esther's chapter runs on guilt, judgment, and the worth of an unborn soul, her stillbirths weighed against conscience and a feared reckoning. A "swan flying out of a swan" and "stillborn falling like snow" carry the Holy Spirit and the day of judgment into her half-dream.
