Definition from research

The novel holds no doctrine. It stages faith as an argument among people who cannot agree. Miss MacIntosh keeps a plain works-faith of "truth to nature," a carpenter God, and no patience for visions. Bonebreaker shouts an apocalypse. Catherine drifts in opium dreams and places her "faith only in the unreal." Spitzer keeps a ritual he half believes. Esther wrestles a God gone silent. Cousin Hannah makes a holy war of suffrage. Young chooses among none of them. She sets them side by side and lets the contradictions stand, so that Christianity here is not one thing but a whole spectrum, a source of strength for one character and of delusion for the next, sometimes on the same page.
