Philosophy: Hinduism

Definition from research

The novel does not argue Hindu thought either, but it leans the same way. Its sense that all beings share one essence, and that the separate self is a passing illusion, echoes the Hindu idea of Brahman, the single reality beneath every apparent thing, and its glancing talk of rebirth touches the same tradition.
