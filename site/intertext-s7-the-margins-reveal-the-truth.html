The Margins Reveal the Truth

Characters: Catherine Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh

Allusions: Iowa, Indiana, and the Midwest · Milton

Definition from research

"The people who do not fit the categories see the categories most clearly. The margins are where the seams show."

Young brings the great into the book through their wounds. Catherine's visitors include "crippled Mr. Alexander Pope" and "blind Milton dictating Paradise Lost to those indifferent daughters" (chapter 2). Miss MacIntosh's scorn for the great men ends in pity: "Oh, my poor Canute who could not stay the tide" (chapter 63).

The keepers of the canon in this novel stand at the margins: a nursemaid, a bus-driver, an Iowa girl who mixes up Venus and Adonis. The essay "The Canon Through Its Wounds" follows this pattern. From the edges, the seams of the inheritance show plainly, and so does its worth.
