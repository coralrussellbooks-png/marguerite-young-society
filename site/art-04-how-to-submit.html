How to Submit

← An Invitation to Work Here · Art When Here Is Enough · The Tension of the Unfinished Cloth →

Contact me through (DM) direct message here on Substack (coralrussell.substack.com).

I will also start a separate Chat thread as well.

Include a brief note about what you are making and why, not a formal artist statement, just an honest account of what the work is trying to do. The note does not have to use the language of Radical Immanence. It just has to be honest.

Work can be submitted as text, image, sound file, video link, or whatever format the work requires. Work that is formally incompatible with Substack formats can be described and linked.

The work belongs to you and will always belong to you. What I will do is share it on Substack, Facebook, and Bluesky as those are the current social media outlets I’m working on. Substack being the largest.

You do not have to be a “professional” anything. There is no deadline. The sentence is still going. The work is still on-going. Send what is ready, knowing that ready does not mean finished.

The only requirement is honesty. The tradition does not ask for excellence in the conventional sense. It asks for work that is trying to be honest about what it is holding. That is enough. That is always enough.

Here is where we are. This is the lineage. This is the invitation. The work is here if you want to make it here.

The following sections are completely optional. If you need inspiration or ideas or a different way of looking at the work, by all means read and use and share. This is not copyrighted in any way.

The Marguerite Young Society is dedicated to the work of Marguerite Young (1908 to 1995).

Everything on this site is free to use and share.
