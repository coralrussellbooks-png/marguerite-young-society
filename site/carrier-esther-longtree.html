Character: Esther Longtree

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Cupid who missed her, Jesus Christ and the virgin birth, the Song of Songs "of the sterility," her imagined baby as Little Tom Thumb, the Ghostly Reaper, lovebirds named Martin Luther and Beau Geste, descent from George Washington's sister, Prince Charming.

Pattern

Imagined motherhood built out of sacred and fairy-tale material. She claims holiness and pedigree at once. Her references name things that do not exist (a baby, a lover) and give grand names to small things (the lovebirds).

Key references (chapters)

Lovebirds (72); Tom Thumb (76); Cupid (76, 79); Song of Songs (77); Jesus Christ (78, 79); Washington's sister (78).

Line from the novel

"the Song of Songs of the sterility" (ch. 77)
