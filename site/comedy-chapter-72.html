Definition from research

Esther Longtree introduces herself with a list of boasts: good tonsils, good teeth, good health, no tumor unless it is benign, and such strength that she can break a fly’s back in the air with a snap of her fingers. The coffee will take a while under the feeble gas flame. She is tired, she explains, only because of her pregnancy, the little shadow boxer, who gives her two hearts.

Old Doc, who comes in to borrow a nickel, has pronounced her the immortal medical case, who will be pregnant until the stars fall and the skies roll up like a scroll on the last day, and who will never die in childbirth because it will be like passing a seed. The comedy is broad and tender at once, and it sits on top of the stillbirths that the coming chapters will uncover.
