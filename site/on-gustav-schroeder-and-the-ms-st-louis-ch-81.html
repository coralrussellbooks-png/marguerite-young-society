On Gustav Schroeder and the MS St. Louis

The German captain who tries to do the right thing and fails is connected in To All My Darlings to Gustav Schroeder, captain of the MS St. Louis, who in 1939 carried 937 Jewish refugees from Germany to Cuba. Cuba refused to admit them. He sailed to Florida; the United States refused entry. He sailed to Canada; Canada refused. He returned to Europe, where the passengers were distributed among four Allied countries: Britain, France, Belgium, the Netherlands. After the fall of France and the Netherlands, many of those passengers were caught in the Nazi net. Historians estimate that approximately 254 of the 937 passengers on the MS St. Louis were killed in the Holocaust. Schroeder did everything he could. It was not enough. He conducted to the last.
