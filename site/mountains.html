Definition from research

Chapter 11 · Queen Maud Mountains

  • Personified as a cold, snobbish, imperious houseguest, symbolizing perhaps a harsh, distant presence.
  • The detail of her five-hundred-mile-long Brussels lace skirt evokes grandeur, coldness, and isolation.

Chapter 30 · Mountains and Deserts

  • Represent challenges, isolation, and spiritual journeys.

Chapter 34 · Mountains and Avalanches

  • Represent obstacles, permanence, and sudden destruction in life and relationships.

Chapter 43 · Storm, Snow, Desert, Mountains

  • Represent the suffragette’s internal struggle and the vast unknown of death.

Chapter 44 · Mountains, Clouds, Icebergs

  • Evoke the harshness and isolation of her inner world and struggles.
  • The iceberg imagery suggests overwhelming, unseen dangers and emotional coldness.

Chapter 45 · Mountain Peaks

  • Obstacles, spiritual heights, isolation, and the vast unknown.

Chapter 46 · Mountains and Snow

  • Mountains stand for the ultimate challenges and heights of spirit and consciousness. Snow and ice symbolize purity, death, and timelessness, as well as the coldness and isolation that often accompany great achievements or sacrifices.

Chapter 47 · Mountains and Snow

  • Death’s tomb, purity, and coldness.
