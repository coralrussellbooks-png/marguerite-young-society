On the segno and the unfinished composition.

In musical notation, a segno (from the Italian for sign) marks a point to which a player returns when they see the instruction dal segno, from the sign. It is the notation for going back, for repeating a section, for the music folding back on itself. Mr. Spitzer's composition is full of segnos: signs that say return here, that mark the places where the music loops rather than progressing. His life is similarly marked by segnos: the places where he returns to the same positions (the deathbed vigil, the missed connection, the timid chess player's refusal to move) rather than advancing. The composition is unfinished not because he lacks the notes but because the music keeps returning to the sign and cannot get past it.
