Chapter 47: The Skirt Over the Wound

← Chapter 46 · Chapter Hubs Home · Chapter 48 →

Story arc: Arc 4 Cousin Hannah

Character hubs: Miss MacIntosh · Joachim Spitzer · Peron Spitzer · Cousin Hannah

Summary

Mr. Spitzer remembers Hannah's last words about the mountains moving, and lays her skirt over her body. He wonders whether her love was that skirt, covering her wound. He reflects on what he owes his twin, on materialism and spirit, on everything in the world seeming conscious, and on the rejected possibility, the life that was never lived. He decides it is better that lost things sink whole than drift scattered.
