What this workbook holds

Index: one row for each reference on each page where it appears, in chapter order. Names that recur as a character or animal name (Falada, Musidora, Lorena) are listed once per chapter.

References A-Z: one row for each reference, with live counts and first and last chapters drawn from the Index sheet.

By Chapter and By Carrier: live counts drawn from the Index sheet.

Reader Connections: texts discussed in earlier volumes that the novel does not name, so they belong under accidental or optional intertextuality.

Corrections: items from the earlier reference list checked against the text.

How pages are counted

The four PDF files carry no printed page numbers. "Page (PDF)" counts straight through all four files, 1 to 1053, in reading order. "Source file" and "File page" locate the same page inside each file. The column "Print edition page" is left blank for the edition chosen for the volume.

How the references were found

Every capitalized name in the novel (about 2,000 distinct forms) was reviewed and sorted into references, invented characters, and ordinary words. The references were then searched through the full text, together with a list of phrases that echo sources without naming them (Bible verses, Shakespeare, Calderon, Virgil, Coleridge, Franklin, nursery rhymes, hymns). Each unnamed allusion was checked in context before it was kept.

Unnamed allusions that do not share wording with a known phrase can only be found by reading. The phrase search catches many, but a reading pass is still needed, and new finds can be added as rows on the Index sheet. The counting sheets will update on their own.

Carrier columns (Phase Two)

Carrier: the character whose voice or mind produces the reference. When one character reports another's visions (Mr. Spitzer watching Catherine's imaginary guests, Catherine dreaming Cousin Hannah's travels), the carrier is the one who imagines or speaks, and the other appears as the secondary carrier.

Carrier basis: "Read" means the row was read in context during Phase Two. All literature, Bible, myth, folklore, song, religion and historical-figure rows were read (about 860). "Auto" rows (mostly geography, events, astronomy and commerce) were assigned by chapter point of view and the names nearest the reference, and should be spot-checked before they are quoted.

Voice: whether the reference sits in narration or inside quoted speech.

Category (provisional): Obligatory is reserved for references the novel builds on (Pilgrim's Progress, Don Quixote, Tantalus, Falada, the Calderon line, and a few others). Most named references start as Optional until Phase Four. Geography is tagged as setting.

Phase Six

Quotation Check lists every quotation used in the volume's essays and in the earlier volumes' essays, checked word for word against the novel's text, with the chapter where each was found. All citations in the volume are by chapter, because page numbers differ between the 1965 Scribner edition and the later two-volume Dalkey Archive edition.
