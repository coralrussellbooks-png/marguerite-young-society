Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Allusions: Revelation and the Apocalypse

Definition from research

"Did his right hand know what his left hand did" turns Matthew 6:3, the charity done in secret, into a charge of hypocrisy against Mr. Spitzer, and quietly foreshadows the doubt about who he really is.

"Which was alpha, and which was omega?" borrows Revelation's title for God to voice the mother's confusion over Mr. Spitzer's identity, beginning and end blurred together.
