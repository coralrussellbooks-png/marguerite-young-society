Definition from research

Miss MacIntosh calls life "a marsh land, a Slough of Despond," borrowing Bunyan's bog of discouragement on the pilgrim's road.

"Am I my brother's keeper?" is Cain's evasion in Genesis 4:9, the denial of responsibility for another.

"Deliver not my soul to a turtle or a crab" echoes the desperate plea of Psalm 22, "deliver my soul," turned toward strange, homely dangers.

"We stumble in the broad daylight which God has lighted, even as He has lighted the darkness" leans on Genesis 1, God dividing light from dark, and finds people erring even in the light.

"Why should light be given to a man who is in misery... whom God hath hedged in" is Job 3, Job's cry that life is a curse to the suffering who long for death and cannot find it.
