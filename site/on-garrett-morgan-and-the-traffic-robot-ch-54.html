On Garrett Morgan and the traffic robot.

The note in To All My Darlings on the traffic robot refers to Garrett Augustus Morgan, the African American inventor who patented the three-position traffic signal in 1923: the precursor to the modern traffic light, with its crucial innovation of a caution interval between stop and go. Morgan, who also invented the safety hood (an early gas mask), sold the rights to his traffic signal to General Electric. Young's shifting Boston, where traffic robots direct the flow of a city in perpetual rearrangement, is presided over by this invention: the device that imposes a brief pause between the end of one state and the beginning of another. The caution signal. The yellow light. This is Mr. Spitzer's interval, written into the infrastructure of the city.
