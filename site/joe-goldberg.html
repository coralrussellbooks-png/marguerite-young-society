Definition from research

Chapter 73

Listed as: Joe Goldberg (Champion Featherweight)

  • A once-powerful boxing champion, now frail and dying of tuberculosis.
  • Writes long, emotional letters to Esther, hoping to be remembered and to claim his child.
  • Represents strength, vulnerability, and the tragic human fight against death.
  • His relationship with Esther is ambiguous, partly forgotten by her but deeply significant to him.
