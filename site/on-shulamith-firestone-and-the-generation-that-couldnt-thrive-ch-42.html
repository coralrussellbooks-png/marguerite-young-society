On Shulamith Firestone and the generation that couldn't thrive.

Firestone published The Dialectic of Sex in 1970, five years after Young's novel appeared. She died in 2012, alone, in her New York apartment, her body not discovered for several days. She was one of the founders of second-wave radical feminism, and her death (the circumstances of it, the obscurity of it, the gap between her contribution and her end) became a kind of painful emblem for the fate of the women who had given everything to a movement that had not yet given them enough in return. Young could not have known Firestone's story. But Cousin Hannah's death is that story's literary antecedent: the warrior who burns everything for the cause, and dies without the world adequately honoring what was burned.
