Bus-driver as Carrier

Part of Intertextual Index, a study of Marguerite Young’s novel Miss MacIntosh, My Darling (1965).
By Coral Russell · Updated October 29, 2026

Intertextual Index Home

Character: Moses Hunnecker

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

Oaths (jumping Jesus, holy Lucifer), Penelope and Ulysses, Argus eyes, Gabriel's horn, Charon's fare, the begats recited as a lament, Solomon and Bathsheba as an oath, Render unto Caesar turned into Render unto Doc, Father Time, Alpha and Omega, Roosevelt.

Pattern

Scripture as profanity and barroom litany. He recites the tribes of Israel for "poor humanity that was dead before it was ever born," the same genealogies Miss MacIntosh uses as a memory drill. He reverses gender roles (Penelope, not Ulysses) and turns sacred formulas into jokes about the doctor.

Key references (chapters)

Oaths (5, 7); Penelope (5); Charon (5, 8); tribes of Israel (7); Render unto Caesar (8); Gabriel (5, 7).

Line from the novel

"he was Penelope weaving this web, not Ulysses" (ch. 5)
