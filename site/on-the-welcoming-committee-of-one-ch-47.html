On the Welcoming Committee of One.

Mr. Spitzer's image of his dead twin forming “a Welcoming Committee of One on the other side” (waiting there, the sole representative of the afterlife, ready to receive his surviving half) is one of the novel's most gently comic and most heartbreaking formulations. The dead twin waiting. The living twin unable to arrive, unable to die, unable to complete the reunion that death would require. Two men who were once one person, separated by the most irreversible of distances, each being the other's unfinished business. The committee meets when Mr. Spitzer arrives. Not before. He is not there yet. He is still in the silk hat, still on this side.
