Definition from research

The Tavern landlord and the Christian hangman are a classic grim double act. The landlord, deaf in one ear and forever "plucking at it" to advertise his listening, insists cheerfully that he will never hang because he is destined to drown, reciting the ships that have gone down under him, "I was on the Titanic. I was on the Lusitania." The hangman, Mr. Weed, arrives shaking because he nearly ran over a dog asleep in the road, and is haunted by dogs generally. Their crowning bit is the murder clue, a "black poker chip" found inexplicably near a severed head, solemnly produced as "Exhibit A." The macabre subject matter is played entirely for the comedy of two grim professionals swapping shop talk.
