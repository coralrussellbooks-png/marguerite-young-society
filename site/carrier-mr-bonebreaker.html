Character: Mr. Titus Bonebreaker

Definition from research

What the cluster holds

The Last Day, Hagar, Eve's fig leaf, Cain and Abel, Nineveh and Jerusalem destroyed, Chicago to be overthrown, the Land of Ham, Hapsburg and Bourbon thrones he dreams of claiming.

Pattern

Scripture as merchandise and threat: he is the "Gospel salesman" who "sold no goods to her." He is also the likely source of Miss MacIntosh's grievance against Constantine, since he preaches that the old creation "was wrecked by the Emperor Constantine."

Key references (chapters)

Last Day (65); Hagar (65); Constantine (65); royal thrones (65).

Line from the novel

"the old creation which was wrecked by the Emperor Constantine" (ch. 65)
