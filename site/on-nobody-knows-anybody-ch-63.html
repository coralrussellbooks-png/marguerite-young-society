On "nobody knows anybody."

This is the chapter's quiet final devastation, offered in Miss MacIntosh's voice after everything else she has revealed. She has just shown Vera who she is (really is, bald head and missing breast and all) and her conclusion is that it doesn't matter, because “people are never quite what they seem” and nobody truly knows anybody. She is not saying connection is impossible. She is saying it is always incomplete, always approximate, always made through the fog. Even now, Vera does not fully know her. Even now.
