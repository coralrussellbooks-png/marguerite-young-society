Argus Panoptes (Ch 5)

From the reading guide, Chapter 5 · Research: Chapter 5

From the guide

Argus Panoptes was a many-eyed giant in Greek mythology. A watchman set to guard things that needed guarding, whose hundred eyes meant he could never fully sleep. When Moses describes his hair as having “burning eyes like the eyes of Argus,” he is imagining it as a kind of perpetual vigilance, a sentinel against the forces that want to diminish him. It is grandiose and sad and entirely human.
