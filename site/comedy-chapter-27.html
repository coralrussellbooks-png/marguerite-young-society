Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

Nothing frightens Mr. Spitzer so much as the loss of his dignity, and he knows, as everyone does, “there is nothing funnier than a fat man slipping on a banana peel.” He is always busy losing weight, even as he gains it, and he fears icy pavements and polished floors. The comedy is that he sees the joke about himself exactly as clearly as the world does.

His precautions are the chapter’s set piece. In case he should ever be found, even with his face altered, he hopes to be recognized by his cape, his silk hat, his rosebud, his many calling cards, an out-dated passport for a journey he has not taken, and a sheet of his silent music, preludes and epilogues to an uncreated work. A stranger once asked him on the street whether he wanted to die, and he said no while shaking his head yes, and was led safely across.
