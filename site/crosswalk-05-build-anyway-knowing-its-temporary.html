Christianity and Religion in MMMD

Young's most startling theological move is to make God himself incomplete. Miss MacIntosh entertains a creator who "left many things uncreated," who began what he could not finish, whose creation is called problematical and whose understanding may never have been his own. Traditional doctrine would flinch at this. Radical immanence recognizes it at once. …
