Characters: Cousin Hannah · Moses Hunnecker

Allusions: Angels and Archangels · Revelation and the Apocalypse

Definition from research

"a hammer struck rock dividing cherubim" sets the angels in stone, split by a blow.

"the infant Moses sleeping in his little boat among the reeds," "hidden away by women from a cruel king," recalls the child saved on the water, and marks Cousin Hannah's childless house by what it lacks.

"the ancient Moses who had led the children of Israel... through desert storms" is the Exodus, Moses guiding his people through the wilderness.

"scallop shells attached to her belt" carry the emblem of Saint James, the pilgrim's badge.

"Where now you see these flowers, there was once a great city which has disappeared," spoken from a cloud, echoes the ruined cities of Scripture and the promise of a city restored, judgment and the New Jerusalem behind it.

"the unmelting snow-flake in the heart of the sea of burning fire" holds a figure of purity untouched in the very lake of fire of Revelation, incorruptible in the midst of judgment.

A "door of stone" recalls the sealed tomb of Easter, the stone that would be rolled away.
