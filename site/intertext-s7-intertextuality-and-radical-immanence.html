Definition from research

The principles gathered under the name radical immanence were not written as a key to Young's novel, and nothing here claims that she held them. They offer a lens. Held up to the patterns traced in the Carrier and Strategy essays, that lens brings certain features of the book into sharp focus. What follows pairs each principle with the references that seem to embody it. The pairings are one reading of the novel, offered as an argument, not as a claim about Young's intentions.
