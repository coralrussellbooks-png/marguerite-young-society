Allusions: Genesis

Definition from research

The twelve apostles and a thirteenth again call up the full table of the Last Supper, Judas among them.

"Omega" is the last letter, God's title as the end of all things, Alpha and Omega.

"Webs of Water" reaches back to the waters of Genesis and creation, and "Bee-belted Earth" glances at the promised land of milk and honey.

A cluster of images, the "Almighty Tumult of Wings," the "Dragonfly Dropping upon Its Silken Cable Line," and "that which flew up through Mary's skirts... the pigeon-colored clouds," all figure the Holy Spirit as a descending dove, the last touching the Annunciation, Mary conceiving by the Spirit.
