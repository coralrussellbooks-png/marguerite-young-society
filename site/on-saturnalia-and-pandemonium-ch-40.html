From the guide

On Saturnalia and Pandemonium.

Young's choice of these two words for Catherine's disordered mind is exact. Saturnalia was the Roman festival when slaves were temporarily freed, roles were inverted, and social order was suspended: joyful and unsettling in equal measure. Pandemonium is Milton's word for Hell's capital in Paradise Lost, coined from the Greek for 'all demons.' Catherine's consciousness contains both: the festival energy of a world freed from its hierarchies, and the demonic energy of a world where all the forces are loose at once. It is exactly as festive and infernal as Young intends.
