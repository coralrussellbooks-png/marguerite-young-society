In the research: Chapter 65, Chapter 66

Definition from research

Chapter 65

  • A passionate, zealous Gospel salesman from Missouri, characterized by his fervor and eccentricity.
  • He preaches about the impending end of the world, salvation, and the need for repentance.
  • He is physically gaunt and stoop-shouldered, with distinctive features like a beaked nose and bright eyes.
  • Despite his religious zeal, he is inconsistent and flawed (e.g., his diet, brushes with police, personal hygiene).
  • He represents religious fanaticism and the human desire for meaning and redemption amid decay and despair.
  • His relationship with Miss MacIntosh is complex: he idealizes her, but ultimately fails to accept her fully.
  • His first name may refer to Titus the Roman emperor from 79 to 81 AD, who was also a celebrated military general. He is known for leading the Roman army to victory in the First Jewish-Roman War, which resulted in the destruction of Jerusalem and its temple. His brief reign as emperor is notable for the eruption of Mount Vesuvius and the opening of the Colosseum.

Chapter 66

Listed as: Mr. Bonebreaker

  • Miss MacIntosh reflects that Mr. Bonebreaker belonged to an old religion where women must kneel before men, similar to the customs of What Cheer, Iowa, where women wash men's feet. She contrasts this with her own situation, noting her baldness and lack of traditionally feminine features, which prevent her from fulfilling such a role.
  • Mr. Bonebreaker seems to represent a lost opportunity for love and connection in Miss MacIntosh's life. He is associated with the idea that she could never kneel before a man, implying a rejection of traditional gender roles and a sense of isolation.
  • It is said that Mr. Bonebreaker fled leaving his umbrella behind.
  • Miss MacIntosh's baldness is a significant factor in her perception of her relationship with Mr. Bonebreaker. She believes her lack of hair and other physical attributes made her unattractive and contributed to the failure of their relationship.
  • Mr. Bonebreaker is mentioned as one of the people who knew Miss MacIntosh was bald.
