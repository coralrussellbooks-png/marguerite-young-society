On the Name Esther

Esther is a Hebrew name meaning “star” or, in some readings, derived from the Persian word for “hidden.” Both meanings apply here. Young has given her a star’s name and situated her in a chapter saturated with celestial imagery: the Milky Way, cold starlight, whirling stars, atmospheric pressure from the cosmos. Esther is also, in the Biblical book that bears her name, a woman who hides her identity and her people’s situation until the moment of crisis, when she acts with extraordinary courage. Young’s Esther hides too: her parents have no idea what she carries. Her secret life (the pregnancies, the stillborn, the shadow boxer) is entirely invisible to the people closest to her.
