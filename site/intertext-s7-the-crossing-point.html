Characters: Joachim Spitzer

Definition from research

"Each person stands where everyone who came before meets everyone who will come after."

The genealogies are the novel's record of those who came before. The bus-driver's lament names those who come after, or who never come: "the poor humanity that was dead before it was ever born" (chapter 7). Between them runs the ferry. Charon appears in the bus-driver's jokes about a dead man with "not a cent with which to pay old Charon" (chapter 5). He is in Mr. Spitzer's purse, where a coin waits "for old Charon when he should make his last passage" (chapter 53). And in the final chapter he is on "the coast of Charon" (chapter 82).

The essay "The Common Underworld" follows that shared picture across every class. The novel begins on a bus crossing a river country in fog, and every reader boards at that crossing.
