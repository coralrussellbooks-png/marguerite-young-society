Characters: Miss MacIntosh

Definition from research

Miss MacIntosh is the great comic corrective to the whole dreaming household, and the humor is in her flat refusal to be impressed by anything. She dismisses chess because it is "the game of kings, an aristocratic pastime employing kings, queens, pawns," and has no patience for pastimes above her station. She plans beach suppers "we might never cook," throws sea shells back into the sea, and is forever "running along the beach, chasing a red ball," a woman so devoted to the practical that even her imagined dream life would be exercise. Her bluntness, her clicking needles, and her creed that "common sense is the finest sense" make her a deadpan foil whose plainness is played, again and again, against a house full of visionaries.
