Characters: Vera Cartwheel · Miss MacIntosh

Definition from research

Vera whispers her secrets into Falada’s drooping ear, and they are funny because they are the secrets of a girl of fourteen: she is an orphan with no mother, Miss MacIntosh is an old witch who keeps her chained in a dark tower, pink is her favorite color, strawberry her favorite ice, and she will have seven stairstep children with long blonde hair. The pony, with his diseased mane and frosty nose, receives it all.

Then she rides him into the house. The tour is grand and absurd at once: past the sleeping marble lions of a Venetian doge, the mosaic emperors like old playing cards, the kings with triple crowns, the black funeral gondola in the indoor fountain, under foam so fine she thinks she should be carrying an umbrella. The comedy of a pony climbing a palace staircase carries the chapter right up to the door it cannot laugh past.
