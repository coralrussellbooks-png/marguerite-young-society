A Note at the End

← Part Eight: Seven-Day Sequences · Radical Immanence Collection · The Crossing Point: The Eleventh Pillar of Radical Immanence →

You have reached the end of this workbook. You may have read it in order or opened it somewhere in the middle. You may have answered every question or none of them. You may have done the practices once, or not at all, or returned to the same page so many times the corner has worn down.

All of that is fine. All of it is enough.

This workbook does not graduate you from anything. It does not mark the completion of a journey. It is just a set of invitations to return to what is already here, and you will need to keep returning. Not because you failed to arrive. Because returning is the practice. It is the whole practice. And it is available to you right now, in this moment, exactly as you are.

You are mid-sentence. So is everyone. The incompletion is not the problem. It is the condition of being alive and still in it.

Come back here whenever you need to. Not to finish it. Just to remember where your life is.

Here Is Enough.
